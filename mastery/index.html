Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Human Biology · 4HB1

Block mastery checks

A short check after every block, with new questions on every attempt.

After every block, check that you have really mastered it. Each check takes new questions from every type in the block (at least 10 questions), marks them instantly and explains every answer. Every retake gives you a new set.

About these questions. They are practice questions written by AI (Claude) for this course. A separate AI review checked each one for correct biology, one right answer and fair wrong options, and fixed any that failed. They are not past-paper questions and not official Pearson material. If you think an answer is wrong, tell your teacher.

  1. Take the block check. If you score 90% or more, you are ready to move on.
  2. Below 90%? Your result lists each type as Secure or Revisit this type. Rework the ones to revisit.
  3. Retake the check until you reach at least 90%. Aim for 100%.

Scores are saved in this browser only.
