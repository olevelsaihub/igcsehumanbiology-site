Past papers and grade guide

Official question papers to sit under exam conditions, and the grade your marks reach.

Every official 4HB1 question paper from 2019–2025, main and regional (R). Print a full pair, Paper 1 and Paper 2 of the same option, sit it under timed conditions (1 hour 45 minutes each), then give it to your teacher to mark against the official mark scheme.

Official mark schemes are for teachers only. Students check individual parts with “Check my answer” on each question page.

Grade guide

After a full pair has been marked against the official scheme, enter the two marks to see the grade they reach on the official Pearson boundaries for 4HB1 (out of 180). Main papers and regional papers have different boundaries. The guide also shows the grade that stays secure with a 10-mark teacher buffer, an internal caution margin, not a Pearson boundary.
