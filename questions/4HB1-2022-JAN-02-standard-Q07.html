Part (b)(i)

(b) (i) A man has the same genotype as person 1 in the pedigree in Diagram 1. A woman has the same genotype as person 3 in the pedigree in Diagram 1. These two people are expecting a child. Calculate the probability that the child would be a boy not showing the Huntington’s condition, giving reasons for your answer.

Part (b)(ii)

(ii) A man has the same genotype as person 2 in the pedigree in Diagram 1. A woman has the same genotype as person 3 in the pedigree in Diagram 1. Explain the possible phenotypes and genotypes, with respect to the Huntington’s condition, of any offspring they produce.
