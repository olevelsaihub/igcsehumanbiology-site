Part (b)

(b) If a person needs a blood transfusion, it is important that they receive a suitable blood group. Explain the effects on a person who receives a blood group that is not suitable.

Part (c)

(c) Explain which blood group or groups can be given to people with each of the other blood groups during a transfusion.
