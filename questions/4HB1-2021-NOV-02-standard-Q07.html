Part (a)(i)

(a) (i) Which of these is the name given to any microorganisms that cause disease? A bacteria B fungi C pathogens D protozoa

Part (a)(ii)

(ii) Explain why it is not possible to treat Ebola using antibiotics.

Part (b)(i)

(i) Calculate the mean rate of change of the bacterial population for antibiotic B during the five hours. Give your answer to 2 significant figures. mean rate of change = ________ AU per hour

Part (b)(ii)

(ii) A bactericidal antibiotic kills bacteria. A bacteriostatic antibiotic prevents bacteria from reproducing. Explain which antibiotic is bactericidal. Use data from the graph to help your answer.

Part (b)(iii)

(iii) Discuss which antibiotic would be better to use to treat any disease caused by the bacteria.
