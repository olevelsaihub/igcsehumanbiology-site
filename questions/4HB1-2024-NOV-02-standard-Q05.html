Part (b)(i)

(b) (i) The condition known as hairy ears is thought to be caused by a mutation of an allele on the Y chromosome. Explain how the genetics of this sex-linked condition differ from the genetics of haemophilia.

Part (b)(ii)

(ii) The diagram is a family pedigree showing the inheritance of the hairy ears mutation. 1 2 3 4 Person 1 is a female without the condition and person 2 is a male with the condition. State the genotype of person 1 and the genotype of person 2. Use H to represent the allele for hairy ears. person 1 person 2

Part (b)(iii)

(iii) Neither person 3 nor person 4 has the condition. Explain why the probability of their next child being a girl without hairy ears is 50%.
