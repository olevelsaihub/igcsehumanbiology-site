Why these types are in the plan
87selected question types
13learning days

The review covered 36 current component papers from 2019–2025.

Current-paper marks were assigned to each part’s sole primary type, and every selected type’s prerequisites were added.

Historical coverage check

18 of 18 current paper pairs cleared the target

The route covered at least 113/180 primary-owned marks on every complete 2019–2025 pair. Each pair stayed at least 10 marks above the higher of its own historical Grade 5 boundary and the matching June 2026 Grade 5 boundary.

This is 100% historical paper-pair coverage, not a 100% probability claim for a future student grade. Accuracy, timing, independent recall and unseen wording still matter.

Completion time

13 learning days plus 6 paper sessions

Study 6 to 7 types per day, then sit three complete Paper 1 + Paper 2 mock pairs as six separate paper sessions. That is 19 active days.

This keeps the olevels.com Pass Plan pace of about 6.6 types per learning day.

Readiness gate

Completion is practice. Fresh papers confirm readiness.

After completing the plan, attempt three fresh, unseen 180-mark Paper 1 + Paper 2 paper pairs under timed conditions. Have each pair independently marked with its official scheme. Grade 5 readiness is supported only when every score is at least 10 marks above that exact pair’s published Grade 5 boundary.

Supporting skills for Human Biology

Use these short bridges when a starting skill needs strengthening.

Reading the question and command words

Tell State, Name, Describe, Explain, Suggest, Compare and Evaluate apart and match the length of the answer to the marks

Calculator, percentages and units

Use a calculator for fractions, decimals and percentages, round sensibly and convert between mm, µm, cm³ and dm³

Reading tables and graphs

Read values from tables, line graphs and bar charts, using the axis scales and units
