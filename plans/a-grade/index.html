Supporting skills for Human Biology

These starting skills support many types. Check them first, and come back to them whenever a type feels hard.

  • Reading the question and command words: Tell State, Name, Describe, Explain, Suggest, Compare and Evaluate apart and match the length of the answer to the marks
  • Calculator, percentages and units: Use a calculator for fractions, decimals and percentages, round sensibly and convert between mm, µm, cm³ and dm³
  • Reading tables and graphs: Read values from tables, line graphs and bar charts, using the axis scales and units
