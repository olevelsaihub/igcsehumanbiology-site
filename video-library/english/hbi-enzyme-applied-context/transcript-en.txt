Large molecules in food must be digested before they can be absorbed. Digest means break down into small, soluble molecules. Soluble means they dissolve in water. An enzyme is a protein that speeds up this breakdown. Absorbed means passed through the gut wall into the blood. Learn the main pairs. Proteases break protein into amino acids, in the stomach and small intestine. Lipase breaks lipids, or fats, into fatty acids and glycerol. Lactase breaks lactose, the sugar in milk, into glucose and galactose.

In a new situation, use five steps. Step one: what type of molecule is it? Step two: name the enzyme and its product. Step three: say where. Step four: is it digested or not, and can it be absorbed? Step five: give the effect on the body. Example one. Some adults lose their lactase. Then lactose stays whole in the gut. It is too large to absorb, so it is not absorbed. Example two. Natural oestrogen, a hormone, is a lipid. As a pill, it is broken down by lipase in the small intestine. So it is not absorbed in its active form. Example three. Chymosin is an enzyme in the stomach of young children, who drink mostly milk. It turns milk protein solid. A solid stays in the stomach longer, so protease has more time to digest it. A general rule: with less of a substrate in the diet, less product is made and absorbed. A substrate is the molecule an enzyme acts on. This picture shows the idea.

Now a real exam question. Phenylketonuria, or PKU, is a genetic disorder. People with PKU cannot break down the amino acid phenylalanine, so it builds up in the blood and brain. Part two: explain why they should follow a low-protein diet. Three marks. Point one: a low-protein diet gives less protein for protease to digest. Point two: protease works in the stomach and small intestine, and breaks protein into amino acids, including phenylalanine. Point three: so fewer amino acids are made. Point four: less phenylalanine is absorbed into the blood, so less of it builds up. Here is the full answer. This answer is by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

A common mistake is writing less phenylalanine with no link to protein digestion. Another is saying lactose is not soluble. It is too large to be absorbed. Do not say the hormone pill is destroyed by acid when the passage says it is a lipid. And do not stop at the enzyme. Finish with absorption and the effect.

Your turn. Insulin is a protein hormone. People with type 1 diabetes must inject insulin. They cannot swallow it as a tablet. Explain why insulin cannot be taken as a tablet. Three marks. Pause here and work it out.

Here is the answer. Insulin is a protein. In the stomach and small intestine, protease digests it into amino acids. Only amino acids are absorbed, so no insulin reaches the blood in its active form. That is why it is injected. So, every time: find the type of molecule. Name the enzyme and product. Say where. Decide if it is absorbed. Give the effect on the body.
