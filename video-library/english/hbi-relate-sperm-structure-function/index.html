Read the narration

A sperm has one job: to reach the egg, or ovum, and join with it. This joining is called fertilisation. Every part of the sperm helps with this job. Here are the parts. The head holds the nucleus, and at its tip is the acrosome, a small sac. Behind the head is the mid-piece, packed with mitochondria. Last is the long tail.

Step one: name the part from its position. Step two: write a chain. Say the feature, then what it does, then how that helps fertilisation. For example: the tail moves from side to side, so the sperm swims to the egg. Step three: the mid-piece is packed with mitochondria. Mitochondria carry out aerobic respiration, which releases energy. Respiration is a reaction in cells that releases energy from glucose. Aerobic means it uses oxygen. The tail uses this energy to move, so the sperm can swim to the egg. Step four: the acrosome holds digestive enzymes. When the sperm reaches the egg, the acrosome releases them. The enzymes digest, or break down, the jelly coat around the egg. This lets the sperm head get in. Step five: the nucleus in the head is haploid. It has one set of chromosomes, 23. Chromosomes are threads of DNA that carry the genes. At fertilisation it fuses with the egg nucleus, which also has 23. The new cell, the zygote, has 46.

Now a real exam question. The diagram shows a human sperm, with parts A, B and C. Part ii asks you to describe the function of part C, for three marks. First, name C from its position. C is the small cap at the very tip of the head. So C is the acrosome. Three marks, so three linked ideas. One: the acrosome releases enzymes when the sperm reaches the egg. Two: the enzymes digest the jelly coat around the egg. Three: this lets the sperm nucleus enter, so fertilisation can happen. Here is a full answer. This answer is by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

A common mistake is to say mitochondria make energy. Energy cannot be made. Say mitochondria carry out respiration, which releases energy for the tail to swim. Also, writing that the acrosome contains enzymes only names the structure. Say what the enzymes do. And they digest the jelly coat, not a cell wall: human eggs have no cell wall.

Your turn. A fertility clinic tests a man's sperm. Many of his sperm have very few mitochondria in the mid-piece. Explain why these sperm may not fertilise an egg. Then explain why the sperm nucleus must have 23 chromosomes, not 46. Pause here and work it out.

Part a: fewer mitochondria means less respiration, so less energy is released. The tail cannot move fast enough or for long enough. So the sperm may not reach the egg. Part b: at fertilisation the sperm nucleus fuses with the egg nucleus, which has 23. Twenty-three plus 23 gives 46, the normal number. With 46 in the sperm, the zygote would have 69, far too many. So, every time: name the part from its position, then give the chain: feature, what it does, and how it helps the sperm reach and fertilise the egg.
