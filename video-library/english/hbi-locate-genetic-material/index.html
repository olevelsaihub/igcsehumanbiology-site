Locate DNA and chromosomes in a human cell

Idea. Look at a human body cell. In the middle is the nucleus. DNA is the molecule that carries genetic information. That is the set of instructions for building and running the body. DNA molecules are very long. They are coiled and packed tightly to make chromosomes. In a body cell, the chromosomes are kept inside the nucleus. So the nucleus contains chromosomes, and chromosomes are made of DNA.

Method. Step one: decide what the question wants. Is it a place, a structure, or a molecule? A place in the cell is the nucleus. A structure is a chromosome. A molecule is DNA. Step two: cross out the wrong options. Organelles are the small parts of a cell, each with a job. Ribosomes make proteins. The cell membrane controls what enters and leaves. Neither holds DNA, and nor does the cytoplasm, the jelly of the cell. In a human cell, only the nucleus holds the chromosomes. Some options look like DNA words. Messenger RNA, or mRNA, is a copy of a gene that leaves the nucleus. It is not part of a chromosome. Ribose is the sugar in RNA. DNA has a different sugar, deoxyribose. Glucose and glycogen are not nucleic acids at all. Step three: write one exact word. Which part of the cell? Nucleus. Which structure? Chromosome. Which molecule? DNA. Do not add a second answer, because it can cancel the mark. Here is a clue. If a diagram shows two strands, or the base thymine, it is DNA. Thymine, T, is found only in DNA. So it is DNA, and in a human cell it is in the nucleus.

Worked example. Now a real exam question. Which structure in a cell contains DNA? There are four options. One mark. Cross out the wrong ones. B, the endoplasmic reticulum, transports proteins. It has no DNA. C, the cell membrane, controls entry and exit. No DNA. D, ribosomes, make proteins. No DNA. A, the chromosome, is made of coiled DNA. So the answer is A. Here is the full reasoning. This answer is by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is choosing mRNA because it sounds genetic, or ribose because it is in a nucleic acid. Chromosomes are made of DNA, and DNA has deoxyribose. Another mistake is the wrong level. If the question asks for the part of the cell, write nucleus, not chromosome. Never write cytoplasm or ribosome.

Your turn. Mature red blood cells have no nucleus. Part a: do mature red blood cells contain chromosomes? Explain your answer. Part b: which of these is found in a chromosome? A, glycogen. B, DNA. C, mRNA. D, ribose. Pause here and work it out.

Answer. Here is the answer. Part a: no. Chromosomes are kept in the nucleus. A red blood cell has no nucleus, so it has no chromosomes, and no DNA. Part b: B, DNA. So, every time: the nucleus contains chromosomes, and chromosomes are made of DNA. Answer at the level the question asks. Cross out RNA, sugars and organelles.
