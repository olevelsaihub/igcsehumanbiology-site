A bacterium is a single cell. It is much smaller than a human cell. On the outside is a cell wall, which supports and protects the cell. Just inside it is the cell membrane, which controls what goes in and out. The cytoplasm fills the cell. A bacterium has no nucleus. Its DNA is one loop, free in the cytoplasm. Many bacteria also have plasmids: small extra rings of DNA. Ribosomes make proteins. A flagellum spins to move the cell. Some have a slime capsule outside the wall.

Now compare a human cell, like a skin cell. It has a cell membrane, cytoplasm and ribosomes. Its DNA is inside a nucleus. It has mitochondria, where respiration releases energy. It has no cell wall. Similarities first. Both cells have a cell membrane, cytoplasm and ribosomes. Both have DNA. In a bacterium it is a loose loop; in a human cell it is in a nucleus. Now differences. A bacterium has a cell wall; a human cell does not. A human cell has a nucleus; a bacterium does not. Bacteria often have plasmids. Human cells have mitochondria. Always say which cell has the structure. Learn one job for each part. The cell wall supports and protects. The membrane controls what enters and leaves. Ribosomes make proteins. DNA holds the genetic code. The flagellum moves the cell.

Here is a real exam question. The diagrams show a bacterial cell and a human skin cell. Give two differences between them. Two marks. Look at the bacterium: a thick outer wall and a long flagellum. The skin cell has no wall. Difference one: the bacterial cell has a cell wall, but the skin cell does not. The skin cell has a clear nucleus. The bacterium has none; its DNA is free in the cytoplasm. That is difference two. Answer by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme. So the full answer is two sentences. Each one names a structure and says which cell has it.

A common mistake is to say a bacterium has no DNA, because it has no nucleus. It does have DNA, as one loop in the cytoplasm. Another mistake is a difference with no direction, like just 'cell wall'. Or saying only one cell has a membrane. Both cells have a membrane.
