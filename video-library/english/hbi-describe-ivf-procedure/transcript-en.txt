Idea. IVF means in-vitro fertilisation. In vitro means in glass. The eggs are fertilised outside the body, in a glass or plastic dish in a laboratory. It helps couples who cannot have a baby naturally. IVF raises the chance of pregnancy in three ways. Many eggs are collected and fertilised. Healthy embryos can be chosen. An embryo is the ball of cells that grows from a fertilised egg. And one or two embryos can be put into the uterus.

Method. Step one: the woman is given two hormones, FSH and LH. A follicle is a small sac in the ovary that holds an egg. FSH, follicle-stimulating hormone, makes several follicles grow, so many eggs mature at once. LH then makes the eggs ripe. Step two: the ripe eggs are collected from the ovaries, using a thin needle. Sperm are collected from the man. Step three: eggs and sperm are mixed in a dish in the laboratory. Sperm fertilise the eggs, and zygotes, or fertilised eggs, form. Step four: the zygotes divide by mitosis into small embryos. The embryos are checked. Step five: one or two embryos are put into the uterus, through a thin tube. An embryo may implant, which means it sinks into the soft lining of the uterus and grows there. If two implant, she has twins.

Worked example. Now a real exam question. IVF can increase the chances of pregnancy. Describe the process of IVF, for four marks. Four marks, so give at least four steps, in time order. There are five: hormones FSH and LH, collect eggs and sperm, fertilise in a laboratory dish, embryos form, and an embryo is put into the uterus. Two details matter most. Say where fertilisation happens: in a dish in the laboratory. And do not forget the last step: the embryo goes back into the uterus. Here is a full answer. Common mistake. A common mistake is to say the eggs are fertilised inside the woman. In IVF they are fertilised in a dish. Another is to name oestrogen and progesterone as the first hormones. The first hormones are FSH and LH. Also, do not stop at fertilisation: the embryo must go into the uterus. And FSH does not make one egg split into twins. It makes several eggs mature.

Your turn. A woman's oviducts are blocked. The oviducts are the tubes from the ovaries to the uterus. Explain why she cannot get pregnant naturally. Explain how IVF can help her. The clinic puts two embryos into her uterus, and she has twins. Are they identical? Pause here and work it out.

Answer. Part a: normally, the sperm meets the egg in the oviduct. If the oviducts are blocked, sperm cannot reach the egg, so the egg cannot be fertilised. Part b: IVF goes around the blocked tubes. The eggs are taken straight from the ovaries and fertilised in a dish. The embryo is put straight into the uterus. Part c: no. The two embryos came from two different eggs and two different sperm, so they have different genes. Identical twins come only when one embryo splits. So remember: hormones, collect, mix in a dish, embryo into the uterus.
