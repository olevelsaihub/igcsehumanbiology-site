A pathogen is a microorganism that causes disease. A microorganism is a living thing too small to see without a microscope. There are four types to know. Bacteria are single cells with no nucleus. Viruses are much smaller, and they can only reproduce inside our cells. A protozoan is a single cell with a nucleus. A fungus grows as thin threads. Not every microorganism is a pathogen. Many bacteria are harmless, so 'pathogen' is the word for all disease-causing organisms.

Step one. Is it caught? A communicable disease is caused by a pathogen, and it can pass from one person to another. A non-communicable disease has no pathogen, so it cannot be caught. Diabetes and heart disease are examples. Step two. Learn which type causes each disease. Bacteria cause cholera, typhoid, tuberculosis and gonorrhoea. Viruses cause Ebola, AIDS, polio, influenza and hepatitis B. The virus that causes AIDS is called HIV. A protozoan causes malaria. A fungus causes athlete's foot. Write only one type of organism in each box of a table. Step four. Do not mix up the cause and the carrier. Malaria is caused by a protozoan called Plasmodium. A mosquito carries it from person to person. The mosquito is the vector, not the cause.

A common mistake is to call all disease-causing organisms 'bacteria'. Many bacteria are harmless, and viruses, protozoa and fungi cause disease too. The general word is pathogen. Another mistake is to name the wrong thing. A mosquito does not cause malaria; a protozoan does. AIDS is a disease, not an organism; it is caused by HIV, a virus.

Your turn. Ravi has cholera. Mei has hepatitis B. Sam has lung cancer, caused by smoking. Name the type of organism that causes Ravi's and Mei's illness. Then suggest why Sam's illness is non-communicable. Pause here and work it out.

Cholera is caused by a bacterium. Hepatitis B is caused by a virus. Sam's lung cancer is not caused by a pathogen. Smoking caused it. So it cannot be passed from one person to another: it is non-communicable. So, every time: a pathogen is a microorganism that causes disease. Communicable means it can be caught.
