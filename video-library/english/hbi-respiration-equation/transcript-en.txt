Respiration is the chemical reaction in every living cell that releases energy from glucose. Glucose is a sugar from our food. Aerobic respiration uses oxygen, and happens mostly in the mitochondria. A mitochondrion is a small part of the cell where this reaction happens. The products are carbon dioxide and water. Carbon dioxide leaves the cell and is breathed out.

Step one: write the word equation. Glucose plus oxygen gives carbon dioxide plus water. The reactants, on the left, are used up. The products, on the right, are made. Step two: swap each word for its formula. Glucose is C six H twelve O six. Oxygen is O two. Carbon dioxide is C O two. Water is H two O. Step three: balance it. Put a six in front of oxygen, carbon dioxide and water. Then count each atom on both sides. Six carbons, twelve hydrogens, and eighteen oxygens on each side. Balanced means the same number of each atom on both sides. Without enough oxygen, muscles respire anaerobically. Anaerobic means without oxygen. In humans, glucose is changed to lactic acid. No carbon dioxide is made, and much less energy is released.

Now a real exam question. Give the balanced chemical symbol equation for aerobic respiration. There are five answer spaces: two before the arrow, and three after it. Three marks. Start with the reactants. Glucose is C six H twelve O six. Then six O two, for six oxygen molecules. Now the products. Six C O two, then six H two O. The fifth space is for energy. Check by counting. Carbon, six and six. Hydrogen, twelve and twelve. Oxygen, eighteen and eighteen. It is balanced. Here is the full answer. It is by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

A common mistake is to write oxygen as a single O, or to leave out the sixes. Then the equation does not balance. Another mistake is to say that anaerobic respiration in humans makes carbon dioxide. In muscles, the only product is lactic acid.

Your turn. During a fast race, a sprinter's leg muscles run short of oxygen. Write the word equation for the respiration in these muscles. Then give two ways it differs from aerobic respiration. Pause here and work it out.

Short of oxygen means anaerobic respiration. In human muscles, glucose gives lactic acid. Differences: no oxygen is used. No carbon dioxide or water is made, but lactic acid is. And much less energy is released from each glucose molecule. Any two of these. So, every time: write the word equation. Swap in each formula. Balance with sixes, and count every atom. And remember, anaerobic in humans gives only lactic acid.
