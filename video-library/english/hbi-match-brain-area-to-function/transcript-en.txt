Idea. The brain has several areas, and each area has its own jobs. Here is the brain cut down the middle, seen from the side, with the front on the left. The large wrinkled top part is the cerebrum, made of two cerebral hemispheres. Under the back of the cerebrum is a smaller wrinkled part, the cerebellum. At the base is the medulla, joined to the spinal cord. In the middle, underneath, is the hypothalamus, with the small pituitary gland hanging below it.

Method. Now match each area to its jobs. The cerebral hemispheres control conscious thought, memory, language, intelligence, and voluntary actions. A voluntary action is one you choose to do. The cerebellum coordinates muscle movement. It controls balance, posture and muscle tone. Posture is how you hold your body. Muscle tone is the slight pull kept in your muscles, even at rest. The medulla controls heart rate and breathing rate. You never have to think about these. The hypothalamus monitors, which means checks, body temperature and the water content of the blood. The pituitary gland releases hormones into the blood, for example ADH. A hormone is a chemical message carried in the blood. When a question says label, draw a ruled line from the name, and end it clearly inside the right area. A line that stops between two areas, or outside the brain, scores nothing. So, in a question, look for the key word. Thought or memory: cerebrum. Balance or posture: cerebellum. Heart or breathing: medulla. Temperature or water: hypothalamus. A hormone released: pituitary.

Worked example. Now a real exam question. The diagram shows a section through a human brain. The table gives four functions. You must write the structure for each one. Four marks, one for each row. Row one, muscle tone and posture. That is part of balance and movement, so the cerebellum. Row two, intelligence and thought. That is the cerebral hemisphere. Row three, monitoring body temperature. That is the hypothalamus. Row four, release of ADH. ADH is a hormone, and the pituitary gland releases it. So the full answer is: cerebellum, cerebral hemisphere, hypothalamus, pituitary gland. This answer is by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is to mix up the cerebrum and the cerebellum, because the names look alike. Think: the cerebellum is the little one at the back, for balance. Another mistake is to give the pituitary for temperature. The hypothalamus monitors temperature. The pituitary only releases hormones. And breathing is the medulla, not the cerebellum.

Your turn. A girl walks along a narrow wall without falling. She remembers a phone number. Her breathing gets faster as she runs home. Her body temperature is checked by her brain. Name areas A, B, C and D, and match each to one of these. Pause here and work it out.

Answer. A is the cerebral hemispheres: remembering the number. B is the cerebellum: balance on the wall. C is the medulla: faster breathing. D is the hypothalamus: checking temperature. So, every time: picture the brain map, find the key word in the function, and match it to one area.
