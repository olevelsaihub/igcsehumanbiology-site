In a healthy eye, the cornea and the lens bend light rays so that they meet exactly on the retina. Then the image is sharp. If the rays meet in front of the retina, or behind it, the image is blurred. There are two common defects. In short sight you see near things clearly, but distant things are blurred. In long sight you see distant things clearly, but near things are blurred.

Short sight. The eyeball is too long, or the lens is too strong. Light is refracted too much, so the rays meet in front of the retina. Distant objects look blurred. The fix is a concave lens, also called a diverging lens. It spreads the rays out a little before they enter the eye, so they now meet on the retina. Long sight. The eyeball is too short, or the lens is too weak. Light from a near object spreads out. The rays would meet behind the retina, so near objects look blurred. The fix is a convex lens, also called a converging lens. It bends the rays inwards before they enter the eye, so they meet on the retina. In astigmatism, the cornea or lens is unevenly curved, so the image is blurred. Special lenses fix it. A cataract is a cloudy lens, from a build-up of protein. Less light gets through, so vision is misty. Surgery replaces the lens. A damaged cornea can be replaced with a healthy donor cornea. This is a corneal transplant. Its normal shape refracts light correctly onto the retina again. The risks are infection, and rejection, when the immune system attacks the donor cornea.

Now a real exam question. The diagram shows a section through an eye. The question asks you to describe a method of treating long sight. Two marks. In long sight, light from near objects would meet behind the retina. The eye does not bend the light enough. So the treatment must add extra bending. Mark one: wear glasses or contact lenses with a convex, or converging, lens. Mark two: it bends the rays inwards before they enter the eye, so they meet on the retina. A common mistake is mixing up short and long sight. Short-sighted people see near things clearly; distant things are blurred. Also, write blurred, not they cannot see. Another mistake is giving the lens type without saying what it does. Say it converges or diverges the rays. And a cataract is a cloudy lens, not a growth on the eye.
