Read the narration

DNA carries a code made of bases, written as the letters A, T, G and C. The order of these bases in a gene is its sequence. Before a cell divides, its DNA is copied. Sometimes there is a change in the bases. A mutation is a change in the sequence of bases in DNA.

There are three types of gene mutation. Look at the original sequence. In a substitution, one base is swapped for a different base. In a deletion, a base is lost. Here one C is lost, and every base after it shifts along. In an insertion, an extra base is added into the sequence. Why does it matter? A mutation changes the gene, making a new allele, a new version of the gene. That allele may code for a different protein. So a characteristic of the organism may change. Most mutations have no effect, but some are harmful and a few are useful. Mutations can happen by chance when DNA is copied. Mutagens are things that raise the rate of mutation. They include ultraviolet light, X-rays and gamma rays, and chemicals in tobacco smoke. Skin is at special risk. Its cells are exposed to ultraviolet light from the Sun, and they divide often. A mutation can make cells divide out of control. This is cancer.

Now a real exam question. Describe what is meant by the term mutation, for two marks. Mark one is what changes. The order of the bases changes. That is the base sequence. Mark two is where. The change happens in DNA. A substitution is a good example, but these two ideas are what count. Explanation by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

A common mistake is to call a mutation a change in a cell, or a disease. That misses the bases and the DNA. Another mistake is to give radiation, or heat, as a cause with no detail. Name a specific mutagen, such as ultraviolet light or X-rays.

Your turn. A sequence of bases changes from G, C, T, T, A, G, to G, C, T, A, G. Name this type of mutation. Then explain why skin cells are more likely than many other cells to become cancerous. Pause here and work it out.

Before, there are six bases: G, C, T, T, A, G. After, there are only five. One T has been lost, so this is a deletion. Skin cells divide often, so their DNA is copied often. They are also exposed to ultraviolet light, a mutagen. So mutations are more likely, and they can cause uncontrolled division, which is cancer. So remember. A mutation is a change in the base sequence. It happens in DNA. Name the type if asked. And for causes, name a specific mutagen.
