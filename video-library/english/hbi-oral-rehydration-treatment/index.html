Explain oral rehydration and treatment of diarrhoeal disease

Read the narration

Cholera is a disease of the gut. It is caused by a bacterium, which is a tiny living cell. The bacteria grow in the intestine, the long tube after the stomach. They make the intestine lose lots of water and salts. So the person has severe diarrhoea: very watery faeces, passed many times a day. Vomiting, or being sick, also removes water and salts. Here, salts means dissolved minerals such as sodium. They are also called ions. When the body has lost too much water, it is dehydrated. Severe dehydration can kill. Ebola causes the same losses. It is caused by a virus, a particle much smaller than a bacterium.

Here is the method. Step one: name the symptom, which is diarrhoea and vomiting. Step two: say what is lost, which is water and salts. This leads to dehydration. Step three: name the treatment. It is oral rehydration therapy, or ORT. Oral means by mouth, and therapy means treatment. ORT is a drink of clean, boiled water with salts and a little sugar dissolved in it. Glucose is the sugar used. Step four: say what ORT does. It replaces the water and salts that were lost. The glucose helps the gut absorb the salts, and the water follows them into the blood. ORT is cheap and simple, with no hospital equipment. Step five: check the pathogen. A pathogen is a microorganism that causes disease. Cholera is caused by a bacterium. An antibiotic is a drug that kills bacteria, so antibiotics help. Ebola is caused by a virus. Antibiotics do not kill viruses, so they do not help.

Now a real exam question. Cholera causes chronic water loss from the body. The question asks you to explain how a person with cholera can be treated. It is worth four marks, so plan at least four ideas. Point one: cholera causes severe diarrhoea, so the person loses a lot of water and salts. Point two: give oral rehydration therapy, a drink of clean water with salts and sugar. Point three: it replaces the water and salts that were lost, so the person does not become dehydrated. Cholera is caused by a bacterium. So point four: give antibiotics. Point five: the antibiotics kill the cholera bacteria. That is five ideas, more than the four marks need. Here is the full answer. This answer is by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

A common mistake is to write only: drink lots of water. Plain water does not replace the salts. Always say that the drink has salts and sugar in it. Another mistake is to say that ORT kills the bacteria. It only replaces what was lost. Antibiotics kill bacteria, and they do not work on Ebola, because Ebola is a virus. Last, read the command word. Treat means help a person who is already ill. Clean water prevents cholera, but it does not treat a sick person.

Your turn. A young child in a clinic has rotavirus, a virus that infects the gut. She has severe diarrhoea and vomiting. Explain how the child should be treated. Then explain why antibiotics will not cure her. Pause here and work it out.

The child loses lots of water and salts in her diarrhoea and vomit, so she becomes dehydrated. Give her ORT, clean water with salts and sugar, by mouth. It replaces the water and salts she has lost. Rotavirus is a virus. Antibiotics kill bacteria, not viruses. So antibiotics will not cure her. ORT keeps her alive while her body fights the virus. So, every time: name the symptom and what is lost. Describe ORT and say that it replaces the loss. Then check the pathogen: antibiotics only for bacteria.
