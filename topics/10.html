Homeostatic mechanisms · Type 1

Identify skin structures and state what each does

Naming skin layers and parts from a diagram or context and giving their roles: protection, sensing, melanin, fat insulation, barrier to infection.

Explain how ADH changes urine volume and concentration

Hypothalamus detection, pituitary release of ADH, collecting duct permeability, and explaining urine differences on hot days, after drinking water or alcohol, or between samples.

Explain how insulin and glucagon control blood glucose

Pancreatic hormones, liver glycogen storage and release, and completing glucose control diagrams, including naming the hormones and glucose as the blood sugar.

Homeostatic mechanisms · Type 8

Explain how skin blood flow and sweating control body temperature

Vasodilation, vasoconstriction, shivering and sweating in hot, cold or exercise conditions, including drawn skin changes and differences in sweat volume between situations or people.
