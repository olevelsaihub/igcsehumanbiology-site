Apply digestive enzyme action to an unfamiliar situation

Novel contexts where digestion, or its absence, changes what is absorbed: oral hormones, PKU, chymosin, lactase loss.

Type 13 Nutrition and energy HBI-ENZYME-APPLIED-CONTEXT

★★☆☆☆ · 4 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Difficult.

A three- or four-step chain must be built from the passage. Needs secure ideas of digestion, absorption and molecule size. No calculation. Every item is a new context (PKU, contraceptive pill, chymosin, lactose intolerance). Links digestion to absorption, genetics and hormones.

Too large; not digested

Write it as

Exam tipNot "not soluble".

Digested by lipase

The method

  1. Find the molecule

    Protein, lipid or carbohydrate?

  2. Name enzyme and product

    For example protease, amino acids.

  3. Say where

    Stomach or small intestine.

  4. Link to absorption

    Digested or stays whole, then the effect.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    No digestion link

    Always bring in the enzyme.

  • ✗
    "Not soluble"

    Lactose is too large to absorb.

  • ✗
    Off topic

    Use the passage.

  • ✗
    Stop at the enzyme

    Add the effect on absorption.

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. People with PKU cannot break down phenylalanine, which is an amino acid.
  2. Where do amino acids come from? Digestion of protein.
  3. Which enzyme? Protease, in the stomach and small intestine.
  4. So less protein eaten means less protein digested by protease, so fewer amino acids, including less phenylalanine, enter the blood.
  5. Three marks: enzyme action, product, location, and less phenylalanine. I will give all four.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

In a low-protein diet there is less protein for protease enzymes to digest in the stomach and small intestine. So fewer amino acids are produced and absorbed, including less phenylalanine. This stops phenylalanine building up in the blood and brain.

How the marks are earned

Marks are given for: less protein digested by protease, the product being amino acids, the site (stomach or small intestine), and the reduced phenylalanine. Any three of these four give full marks.
