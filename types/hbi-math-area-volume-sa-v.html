Knowledge card

Calculate area, volume and surface area to volume ratio

Calculation

A cube has 6 faces. Surface area = 6 × side², volume = side³, and SA : V is written as n : 1. Smaller cubes have a larger SA : V.

The method

  1. Side

    Write it with its unit.

  2. Surface area

    One face × 6.

  3. Volume

    Side × side × side.

  4. Ratio

    SA ÷ V, written n : 1.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong face count

    A cube has 6 faces.

  • ✗
    Side × 3

    Volume is side cubed.

  • ✗
    Ratio upside down

    SA goes first.

  • ✗
    Diameter in πr²

    Halve it to get r.

Worked explanation

  1. The cube has a side length of 2 mm.
  2. One face = 2 x 2 = 4 mm2. There are 6 faces, so surface area = 6 x 4 = 24 mm2.
  3. Volume = 2 x 2 x 2 = 8 mm3.
  4. SA : V = 24 : 8. Divide both by 8: 3 : 1.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Surface area = 6 x 2 x 2 = 24 mm2. Volume = 2 x 2 x 2 = 8 mm3. SA : V = 24 : 8 = 3 : 1

How the marks are earned

One mark for the surface area of 24 mm2. One mark for the volume of 8 mm3. One mark for 3 : 1. A correct final answer alone scores all three.

Calculation check

  • 6 × 2 × 2 = 24
  • 2 × × 3 = 8
  • (6 × 2 × 2) ÷ (2 × × 3) = 3.0
