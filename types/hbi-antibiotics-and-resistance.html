Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Human Biology · 4HB1

State what antibiotics treat and explain how resistance develops

What antibiotics are and where they come from, why they fail against viruses and fungi, and selection of resistant strains such as MRSA and its consequences.

Type 13 Disease HBI-ANTIBIOTICS-AND-RESISTANCE

★★★★☆ · 8 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

Resistance answers need a multi-step selection sequence in the right order. Natural selection acting on bacteria is abstract for many students. Data tables appear as context but no calculation is needed for these parts. Contexts such as MRSA death data or typhoid strains vary. Links microorganism types, genetics (mutation) and evolution.

Knowledge card

State what antibiotics treat and explain how resistance develops

Written

Antibiotics only act on bacteria. Resistance starts with a random mutation; the antibiotic then selects the resistant bacteria, which survive and reproduce.

4

The method

  1. Check the pathogen

    Bacterium, virus or fungus?

  2. State the target

    Antibiotics act on bacteria only.

  3. Resistance order

    Mutation, non-resistant die, resistant survive, reproduce, pass on the gene.

  4. Cause and result

    Overuse or unfinished courses; harder to treat and spreads.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    "Immune" bacteria

    Bacteria become resistant, not immune.

  • ✗
    Antibiotic causes mutation

    The mutation is random and comes first.

  • ✗
    Stopping at survive

    Add that they reproduce and pass on resistance.

Worked explanation

  1. 'Explain why it is not possible', 2 marks: two linked ideas.
  2. What causes athlete's foot? A fungus.
  3. What do antibiotics act on? Bacteria only.
  4. Link them: antibiotics do not kill fungi, so they cannot cure athlete's foot.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Athlete's foot is caused by a fungus, not a bacterium. Antibiotics only kill bacteria; they have no effect on fungi, so they cannot cure athlete's foot.

How the marks are earned

One mark for saying a fungus causes athlete's foot. One mark for saying antibiotics work only on bacteria, or that they do not kill fungi.
