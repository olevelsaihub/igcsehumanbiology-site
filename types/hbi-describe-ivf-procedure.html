  2. Collect

    Eggs from the ovaries, sperm from the man.

    Say where: the laboratory.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Fertilised inside

    It happens in a dish.

  • ✗
    Wrong hormones

    FSH and LH, not oestrogen.

  • ✗
    Missing transfer

    Put the embryo back in the uterus.

  • ✗
    FSH makes twins

    It causes several eggs to be released.

Worked explanation

  1. Four marks and 'describe the process', so I list at least four steps in order.
  2. Step 1: the woman gets FSH and LH so several eggs mature.
  3. Step 2: the eggs are taken from her ovaries and sperm is taken from the man.
  4. Step 3: they are mixed in a dish in a laboratory so the eggs are fertilised.
  5. Step 4: the fertilised eggs grow into embryos.
  6. Step 5: one or two embryos are put into her uterus to implant.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

The woman is given the hormones FSH and LH so that several eggs mature in her ovaries. The eggs are removed from the ovaries, and sperm is collected from the man. The eggs and sperm are mixed in a dish in a laboratory, where sperm fertilise the eggs. The fertilised eggs divide and develop into embryos. One or two embryos are then placed inside the woman's uterus, where they can implant.

How the marks are earned

Any four of: FSH and LH given; eggs and sperm collected; fertilisation in a laboratory dish; embryos develop; embryo placed in the uterus.
