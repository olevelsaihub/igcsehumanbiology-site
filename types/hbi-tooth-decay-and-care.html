Explain how tooth decay happens and justify ways to prevent it

Written

Bacteria in plaque respire sugar and release acid, which dissolves the enamel. Prevent decay by cutting sugar and strengthening enamel.

Fill each link from sugar to cavity.

  2. Lactic acid is released

Bacteria respire the sugar

Write it as
Bacteria in plaque feed on the sugar and respire it.

Exam tipBacteria do not "eat the teeth".

Lactic acid is released

Write it as
The respiration releases lactic acid.

Exam tipSugar itself does not dissolve teeth.

Acid dissolves enamel

Write it as
The acid dissolves the enamel, then the dentine, making a cavity.

Write it as
Less sugar means less food for bacteria, so less acid.

Stronger enamel

4

The method

  1. Sugar

    Start with sugar left on the teeth.

  2. Bacteria

    They use the sugar in respiration.

  3. Acid

    Respiration releases lactic acid.

  4. Enamel

    The acid dissolves enamel, then dentine.

  5. Prevention

    Give each method with its reason.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Sugar dissolves teeth

    The acid does the damage.

  • ✗
    Bacteria eat teeth

    They respire sugar.

  • ✗
    Fluoride kills bacteria

    It strengthens enamel.

  • ✗
    Same method twice

    Two methods need two different ideas.

Worked explanation

  1. Four marks, so four linked steps.
  2. Step 1: bacteria on the teeth feed on sugar left from food.
  3. Step 2: they use it in respiration for energy.
  4. Step 3: this produces lactic acid.
  5. Step 4: the acid dissolves the enamel.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Bacteria in plaque feed on sugar from food left on the teeth. They use the sugar in respiration to release energy. This produces lactic acid. The acid dissolves the enamel of the tooth, causing decay.

How the marks are earned

One mark each for: bacteria using food or sugar on the teeth, using it for respiration, making lactic acid, and the acid dissolving enamel.
