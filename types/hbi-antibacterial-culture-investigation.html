Interpret antibacterial culture investigations

Petri dish and growth-graph investigations of antibiotics or bacterial growth: comparing inhibition zones or counts, variables and safe incubation.

Type 12 Disease HBI-ANTIBACTERIAL-CULTURE-INVESTIGATION

★★☆☆☆ · 3 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

Students must choose, justify from data and explain; discuss items weigh two options. Clear zones and bactericidal versus bacteriostatic are concrete once seen. Comparing zone sizes, graph trends or growth diameters needs careful data reading. Contexts include garlic extract in milk and unfamiliar terms defined in the stem. Links diffusion, antibiotics and practical method.

4

The method

  1. Make the choice

    Name the antibiotic or dish.

  2. Quote the evidence

    The larger clear zone, or the falling or level line.

  3. Explain it

    It diffused into the agar and killed bacteria, or stopped reproduction.

  4. Compare dishes

    Check what was added and the start numbers.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Smaller zone is better

    The wider clear zone is more effective.

  • ✗
    Choice only

    Add the evidence and the reason.

  • ✗
    Level means killed

    A level line means not reproducing.

  • ✗
    Control with growth

    Bacteria may already be in milk or the air.

Worked explanation

  1. Look at the diagram: the clear zone around disc X is much wider than the clear zone around disc Y.
  2. Decision: X is more effective. But the scheme gives no mark for just naming X, so I need three reasons.
  3. Evidence: the clear zone around X is larger.
  4. Mechanism: the antibiotic diffused out of the filter paper into the agar.
  5. Meaning of the clear zone: bacteria there have been killed, so X killed bacteria over a bigger area.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Antibiotic X is more effective. The clear zone around the X disc is larger than the clear zone around the Y disc. The antibiotic diffused out of the filter paper disc into the agar. In the clear zone the bacteria have been killed, so X killed bacteria over a larger area than Y.

How the marks are earned

Naming X earns nothing by itself. Marks go to: X having the bigger clear zone; the antibiotic diffusing out of the disc; bacteria being killed in the clear area.
