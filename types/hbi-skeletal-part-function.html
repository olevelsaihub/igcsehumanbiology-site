Knowledge card

State the function of a skeletal or joint part and explain effects of damage

Written

Ligaments join bone to bone, tendons join muscle to bone, cartilage reduces friction and synovial fluid lubricates. Without cartilage, bones rub and the joint is painful.

1

Match the part to its job

Say the job first, then tap the ? to check.

4 parts to find

What does each part do?

  1. Joins bone to bone
  2. Joins muscle to bone
  3. Reduces friction
  4. Lubricates the joint

Joins bone to bone

Write it as
Ligaments join bone to bone and hold the joint together.

Exam tipNot muscle to bone.

Joins muscle to bone

Write it as
Tendons join muscle to bone and pass on the pull of the muscle.

Exam tipTendons do not stretch.

Reduces friction

Write it as
Cartilage reduces friction and absorbs shock.

Lubricates the joint

Write it as
Synovial fluid lubricates the joint so it moves easily.

Exam tipIt does not hold bones together.

4

The method

  1. Identify

    Use the label, letter or position.

  2. Give the job

    Bone to bone, or muscle to bone.

  3. Second idea

    For two marks, add a different idea.

  4. Damage

    Say what the part does, then the result.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Ligament and tendon

    Ligament is bone to bone; tendon is muscle to bone.

  • ✗
    Tendons stretch

    Tendons do not stretch.

  • ✗
    Cartilage "protects"

    Say friction or shock.

  • ✗
    Fluid holds bones

    It lubricates; ligaments hold.

Worked explanation

  1. There are 4 marks for two parts, so I need two ideas for tendons and two for cartilage.
  2. Tendons: they attach a muscle to a bone. They are not stretchy, so when the muscle contracts they pass on its pull to the bone.
  3. Cartilage: it is smooth, so it reduces friction between bone ends. It also absorbs shock.
  4. A second cartilage idea: it lets the joint move smoothly. I will include it too, in case 'reduces friction' and 'absorbs shock' count as one point.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Tendons: they attach muscle to bone, and they transfer the pulling force of the muscle to the bone so the bone moves. Cartilage: it reduces friction between the ends of the bones and acts as a shock absorber, so the joint can move smoothly.

How the marks are earned

Two marks for tendons: joining muscle to bone, and passing on the force or pull. Two marks for cartilage: reducing friction or absorbing shock, and allowing smooth movement.
