Make alginate beads and give uses and advantages of immobilised enzymes

Written

An immobilised enzyme is trapped in alginate beads. It can be reused and does not contaminate the product.

Sodium alginate and enzyme

Dissolve sodium alginate in water, then add the enzyme.

4

The method

  1. Name the pair

    Lactase and lactose, or sucrase and sucrose.

  2. Make the beads

    Alginate with enzyme, drip into calcium chloride, rinse.

  3. Advantages

    Give different ideas, one per mark.

  4. Cost

    Link reuse to buying less enzyme.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Enzyme used up

    Enzymes are not used up; they are reused.

  • ✗
    Vague "better"

    Give a reason for each advantage.

  • ✗
    Same idea twice

    Reusable and used again is one idea.

  • ✗
    Wrong pair

    Lactose goes with lactase.

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. The question compares immobilised enzymes with free enzymes and wants two advantages.
  2. Advantage 1: the enzyme stays in the beads, so it can be used many times.
  3. Advantage 2: the enzyme does not mix with the product, so the product is pure.
  4. I have two different ideas for two marks.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

1 The enzyme is held in the beads, so it can be used again and again, which saves money. 2 The enzyme does not get mixed into the product, so the product is pure and does not need to be cleaned.

How the marks are earned

Any two different ideas score: reuse of the enzyme, no enzyme in the product, greater stability (to temperature or pH), or a justified cost saving.
