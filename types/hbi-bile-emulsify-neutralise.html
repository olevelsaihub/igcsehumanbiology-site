Explain where bile comes from and how it aids fat digestion

Bile production in the liver, storage in the gall bladder, emulsification of fat and neutralising stomach acid, including liver damage and bile-free tubes.

Type 12 Nutrition and energy HBI-BILE-EMULSIFY-NEUTRALISE

★★★☆☆ · 6 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

Function answers are two-step links; cirrhosis needs a longer chain. Emulsification and surface area are often confused with digestion. No calculation. Disease and practical contexts vary. Combines liver, enzyme and pH ideas.

Explain where bile comes from and how it aids fat digestion

Written

Bile is made in the liver and stored in the gall bladder. It emulsifies fat for a larger surface area and is alkaline, so it neutralises stomach acid.

Worked explanation

  1. A is the gall bladder and the fluid is bile.
  2. Three marks. Function 1: bile emulsifies fat into small droplets.
  3. Link: this gives a larger surface area for lipase to work on.
  4. Function 2: bile neutralises the acid from the stomach.
  5. I have four ideas for three marks, which is safe.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Bile emulsifies fats, breaking large drops into many small droplets. This increases the surface area so lipase can digest the fat faster. Bile is also alkaline, so it neutralises the acid coming from the stomach.

How the marks are earned

Marks are given for emulsifying fat into small droplets, larger surface area, this helping lipase act, and neutralising stomach acid. Any three score full marks.
