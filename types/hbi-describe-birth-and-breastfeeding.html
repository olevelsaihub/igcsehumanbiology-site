Knowledge card

Describe labour and the advantages of breastfeeding

Written
  • Labour starts when the muscles of the uterus wall begin strong, regular contractions.
  • Oxytocin, released from the pituitary gland, makes the uterus muscles contract during labour.
  • The contractions widen (dilate) the cervix and then push the baby down and out through the vagina.
  • Oxytocin also makes milk flow from the breasts when the baby feeds, and it helps the mother and baby bond.
  • Breast milk contains antibodies from the mother, which give the baby passive immunity and fewer infections.
  • Breast milk has the right balance of nutrients for the baby, at the right temperature, and is free and sterile. It also contains hormones that help the baby develop.
  • Breastfeeding helps emotional bonding and is linked to lower risk of obesity, diabetes and allergies later.
  • Birth in order: the uterus muscles contract, the amniotic sac breaks, the cervix dilates, the baby is pushed out head first through the vagina, the umbilical cord is tied and cut, and the placenta comes out as the afterbirth.

Before you begin

  • Muscles contract to cause movement.
  • Antibodies are made by lymphocytes and protect against pathogens.
  • The structure of the uterus and cervix.
1

The method

  1. For labour, name the muscle (uterus wall) and its action (contracts).

    Why: The contraction of the uterus muscle is the first credit point.

  2. Then give the result: the cervix widens and the baby is pushed out.

    Why: The effect on the cervix is a separate mark.

  3. For oxytocin, list each different job: contractions in labour, milk release, bonding.

    Why: Each different function is one mark, so a list of distinct jobs fills the marks.

  4. For breastfeeding, give different advantages: antibodies, nutrient balance, bonding.

    Why: Repeating one idea in new words scores only once.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Saying the uterus muscles 'relax' to push the baby out.
  • ✗
    Saying the cervix contracts to push the baby out; it widens.
  • ✗
    Writing 'breast milk is healthier' with no reason such as antibodies or nutrient balance.
  • ✗
    Repeating the same advantage twice in different words.
  • ✗
    Choosing progesterone increase as a change during birth.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

Worked explanation

  1. The hormone is oxytocin and there are 3 marks, so I need three separate ideas.
  2. Main job: it makes the uterus muscles contract.
  3. When: during labour (childbirth).
  4. Another job: it makes milk be released from the breasts when the baby feeds.
  5. A fourth idea in case one is weak: it helps emotional bonding between mother and baby.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Oxytocin makes the muscles of the uterus wall contract. This happens during labour, so the baby is pushed out. Oxytocin also stimulates the release of milk from the breasts (lactation). It also helps emotional bonding between the mother and her baby.

How the marks are earned

Up to three of: uterus muscles contract; during labour or childbirth; causes milk to be released; emotional bonding. Each separate idea earns one mark.
