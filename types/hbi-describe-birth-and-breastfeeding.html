4

The method

  1. Name the muscle

    The uterus wall, and its action: contracts.

  2. Give the result

    The cervix widens and the baby is pushed out.

  3. List oxytocin jobs

    Contractions, milk release, bonding.

  4. Breastfeeding

    Different advantages: antibodies, nutrients, bonding.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Muscles relax

    They contract.

  • ✗
    Cervix contracts

    It widens.

  • ✗
    "Healthier"

    Add antibodies or nutrient balance.

  • ✗
    Same advantage twice

    Give different points.

Worked explanation

  1. The hormone is oxytocin and there are 3 marks, so I need three separate ideas.
  2. Main job: it makes the uterus muscles contract.
  3. When: during labour (childbirth).
  4. Another job: it makes milk be released from the breasts when the baby feeds.
  5. A fourth idea in case one is weak: it helps emotional bonding between mother and baby.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Oxytocin makes the muscles of the uterus wall contract. This happens during labour, so the baby is pushed out. Oxytocin also stimulates the release of milk from the breasts (lactation). It also helps emotional bonding between the mother and her baby.

How the marks are earned

Up to three of: uterus muscles contract; during labour or childbirth; causes milk to be released; emotional bonding. Each separate idea earns one mark.
