Knowledge card

Name tooth types and tissues, state their jobs and compare tooth sets

Written

Each tooth has layers from outside in: enamel, dentine, pulp cavity. Each tooth type has one job: cut, tear or grind.

1

Name it, then give its job

Say it first, then tap the ? to check.

4 names to find

Work from the outside in. Name each layer.

  1. Enamel
  2. Dentine
  3. Pulp cavity
  4. Nerve

Enamel

Write it as
Enamel is the hard outer layer of the crown.

Exam tipNot "bone".

Dentine

Write it as
Dentine is the softer layer under the enamel.

Exam tipDo not swap enamel and dentine.

Pulp cavity

Write it as
The pulp cavity holds nerves and blood vessels.

Exam tipNot "the root".

Nerve

Write it as
A nerve (sensory neurone) detects pain and temperature.

  1. Cut and bite
  2. Tear and grip
  3. Grind and crush

Cut and bite

Write it as
Incisors cut and bite food.

Tear and grip

Write it as
Canines tear and grip food.

Exam tipCanines do not grind.

Grind and crush

Write it as
Molars (and premolars) grind and crush food.

Exam tipMolars do not cut.

4

The method

  1. Outside in

    Enamel, dentine, pulp cavity, then the nerve in the root.

  2. Shape

    Flat top with cusps and two roots is a molar.

  3. One action word

    Cut, tear or grind: one job per mark.

  4. Compare both

    Adults have premolars; milk teeth do not.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Enamel and dentine swapped

    Enamel is the hard outer layer.

  • ✗
    Pulp called the root

    The pulp cavity holds the nerve; it is not the nerve.

  • ✗
    Jobs mixed up

    Canines tear; molars grind.

  • ✗
    "Adults have more teeth"

    Give a detail, such as premolars.

Worked explanation

  1. Work from the outside in.
  2. A points to the thin white outer layer of the crown: enamel.
  3. B points to the shaded layer under it: dentine.
  4. C points to the central space with branching vessels: pulp cavity.
  5. D points to a line running out of the root: a nerve.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

A: enamel

B: dentine

C: pulp (pulp cavity)

D: nerve

How the marks are earned

One mark for each correct name. A sensory neurone is also accepted for D.
