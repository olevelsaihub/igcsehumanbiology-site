Knowledge card

Explain oral rehydration and treatment of diarrhoeal disease

Written
  • Diseases such as cholera and Ebola cause severe diarrhoea and vomiting.
  • Diarrhoea and vomiting remove large amounts of water and salts (ions such as sodium and potassium) from the body, so the person becomes dehydrated. Severe dehydration can kill.
  • Oral rehydration therapy (ORT) is a drink of clean (boiled) water with salts (electrolytes, ions such as sodium) and some sugar dissolved in it.
  • ORT replaces the lost water and salts. The glucose helps the gut absorb the salts and water.
  • ORT is cheap and simple, and it can be given by mouth without hospital equipment.
  • Cholera is caused by a bacterium, so antibiotics can also be given to kill it. Ebola is caused by a virus, so antibiotics do not help.

Before you begin

  • Cholera is bacterial; Ebola is viral.
  • Water and ions are absorbed in the intestines.
  • Antibiotics kill bacteria but not viruses.
1

The method

  1. Name the symptom that causes the loss: diarrhoea and vomiting.

    Why: The scheme gives a mark for the symptom.

  2. Say what is lost: water and salts, leading to dehydration.

    Why: This is the reason ORT is needed and is a separate mark.

  3. Describe ORT: clean water with salts (and sugar), drunk by mouth.

    Why: Naming ORT and its contents earn marks.

  4. Say what ORT does: it replaces the lost water and salts.

    Why: Replacement is the key explanation mark.

  5. If the pathogen is a bacterium, add antibiotics to kill it.

    Why: Antibiotics earn marks for cholera, not for Ebola.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Writing only 'drink lots of water' with no mention of salts.
  • ✗
    Saying ORT kills the bacteria. It only replaces what was lost.
  • ✗
    Suggesting antibiotics for Ebola, which is caused by a virus.
  • ✗
    Giving prevention measures (clean water supply, sanitation) when the question asks for treatment.

Worked explanation

  1. 'Explain how... can be treated', 4 marks: I need four ideas.
  2. Why is treatment needed? Cholera gives severe diarrhoea, so water and salts are lost.
  3. Treatment 1: oral rehydration therapy, a drink of salts and sugar in clean water.
  4. What it does: it replaces the water and salts that were lost.
  5. Treatment 2: cholera is caused by a bacterium, so antibiotics can be used to kill it.
  6. Count the ideas: loss, ORT, replacement, antibiotics, killing bacteria. That is five, enough for four marks.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Cholera causes severe diarrhoea, so the person loses a lot of water and salts from the body. The person is given oral rehydration therapy: a drink of clean water with salts and sugar dissolved in it. This replaces the water and salts that have been lost, so the person does not become dehydrated. The person can also be given antibiotics, which kill the cholera bacteria.

How the marks are earned

Marks for: naming oral rehydration therapy; replacing fluid, salts or sugar; the reason (water and salts lost through diarrhoea or vomiting); using antibiotics; antibiotics killing the bacteria. Any four give full marks; the answer has all five.
