Explain how a blocked coronary artery damages the heart

Causes and consequences of narrowing or blocking arteries supplying heart muscle or other tissue.

Knowledge card

Explain how a blocked coronary artery damages the heart

Written

A blocked coronary artery means less blood, so less oxygen and glucose reach the heart muscle. Less aerobic respiration releases less energy, and the muscle can die.

5 links to find

Fill each link from blockage to heart attack.

  1. Deposit narrows the artery
  2. Less blood to the muscle
  3. Less oxygen and glucose
  4. Less energy released

Deposit narrows the artery

Write it as
A fatty deposit (or clot) narrows or blocks the coronary artery.

Less blood to the muscle

Write it as
Less blood reaches the heart muscle beyond the block.

Exam tipThe heart muscle, not the chambers.

Less oxygen and glucose

Write it as
Less oxygen and glucose are delivered to the muscle cells.

Less energy released

Write it as
Less aerobic respiration, so less energy is released.

Exam tipRespiration releases energy, never makes it.

Cells die: heart attack

Write it as
The muscle cells may die, causing a heart attack.

What raises the risk?

  1. More blood cholesterol
  2. More fatty deposits

More blood cholesterol

Write it as
Raises blood cholesterol, which builds fatty deposits (plaques) in the artery wall.

More fatty deposits

The method

  1. Cause

    A deposit or clot narrows or blocks the artery.

  2. Less blood

    Less blood flows to the heart muscle.

  3. Less delivered

    Less oxygen and glucose arrive.

  4. Respiration

    Less aerobic respiration, so less energy is released.

  5. Result

    Muscle cells die; heart attack.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    "Makes energy"

    Write releases energy.

  • ✗
    Chambers blocked

    Coronary arteries feed the muscle itself.

  • ✗
    Only "heart attack"

    Give the whole chain.

Worked explanation

  1. The diagram shows a fatty deposit inside an artery that supplies the heart muscle. The question asks how this causes heart disease.
  2. Link 1: the deposit narrows the artery, so blood flow is restricted.
  3. Link 2: so less blood reaches the heart muscle cells.
  4. Link 3: these cells get less oxygen and glucose.
  5. Link 4: so less aerobic respiration happens in the heart muscle.
  6. Link 5: the muscle cells can die, causing angina or a heart attack. Five links for 4 marks.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

The fatty deposit narrows the artery, so the flow of blood is reduced. Less blood reaches the heart muscle tissue. This means less oxygen and glucose are delivered to the heart muscle cells. So the cells carry out less aerobic respiration and release less energy. The heart muscle cells may die, causing a heart attack.

How the marks are earned

Marks go for: reduced or restricted blood flow; to the heart muscle or its cells; less oxygen or glucose; less aerobic respiration; and the muscle dying, angina or a heart attack. Four of these five give full marks.
