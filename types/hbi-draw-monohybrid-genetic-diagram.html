Draw a genetic diagram for a monohybrid or sex-linked cross

Full crosses for autosomal, codominant blood group, X-linked conditions and sex determination by X and Y chromosomes.

Type 16 Reproduction and heredity HBI-DRAW-MONOHYBRID-GENETIC-DIAGRAM

★★★★★ · 10 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Difficult.

Parent genotypes often have to be deduced first from a pedigree or description. Needs dominance, co-dominance and sex linkage in one layout. Simple ratios and probabilities. New conditions (white forelock, PKD, DMD) use the same method. Combines symbols, pedigrees, gametes and phenotypes.

4

The method

  1. Parents

    Write both genotypes with the given letters.

  2. Gametes

    One allele each, labelled.

  3. Square

    Combine every gamete pair.

  4. Phenotypes

    Match each genotype; add sex if sex-linked.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Gamete Aa

    Split it into A and a.

  • ✗
    Missing X and Y

    Sex-linked crosses need them.

  • ✗
    Unlabelled square

    Label parents and gametes.

Worked explanation

  1. Sickle cell anaemia is recessive. Choose symbols: A for the normal allele, a for the sickle cell allele.
  2. From the pedigree, people 7 and 8 do not have the condition but have an affected parent or child, so each must be a carrier, Aa.
  3. Each parent makes two kinds of gamete: A and a.
  4. Combine them in a Punnett square: AA, Aa, Aa, aa.
  5. Write phenotypes: AA unaffected, Aa unaffected carrier, aa has sickle cell anaemia.
  6. So there is a 1 in 4 chance the child has sickle cell anaemia.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Key: A = normal allele, a = sickle cell allele.

Parent genotypes: person 7 = Aa, person 8 = Aa

Gametes: from 7: A or a; from 8: A or a

Punnett square:

| A | a

A | AA | Aa

a | Aa | aa

Offspring genotypes and phenotypes:

AA = no sickle cell anaemia

Aa (two of the four) = no sickle cell anaemia, but a carrier

aa = has sickle cell anaemia

Chance of an affected child = 1 in 4 (25%).

How the marks are earned

One mark each for: both parents shown as heterozygous; the gametes A and a from each; the three offspring genotypes in a 1:2:1 pattern; each genotype matched to its phenotype (normal, carrier, sickle cell). A Punnett square with no labels can score at most two marks for the gametes and offspring rows.

Calculation check

  • 1 ÷ 4 × 100 = 25.0
