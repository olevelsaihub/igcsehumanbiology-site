Compare blood entering and leaving the kidney

Written

Compared with the renal artery, blood in the renal vein has less urea, less oxygen and more carbon dioxide. Always say which vessel has more or less.

4 differences to find

Does the renal vein have more or less?

  1. Less urea
  2. Less oxygen
  3. More carbon dioxide
  4. Slightly less glucose

Less urea

Why
Urea is filtered out and removed in urine.

Exam tipNever "more urea".

Less oxygen

Why
Kidney cells use oxygen in respiration.

More carbon dioxide

Why
Kidney cells produce it in respiration.

Slightly less glucose

Why
Kidney cells use some in respiration.

Exam tipNot "no glucose": it is reabsorbed.

4

The method

  1. In and out

    Renal artery in, renal vein out.

  2. Compare

    Write "the renal vein has less urea than the renal artery".

  3. Different substances

    Urea, oxygen, carbon dioxide, glucose.

  4. Reason

    Urea is excreted; oxygen is used in respiration.

  5. Check the vessels

    Whole kidney, or one glomerulus?

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    No vessel named

    Say which has more or less.

  • ✗
    "No glucose"

    Glucose is reabsorbed, so a little less.

  • ✗
    Repeating an idea

    "Less urea" and "less waste" is one point.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. The renal artery brings blood in and the renal vein takes it out.
  2. Urea: removed into urine, so less in the renal vein.
  3. Oxygen: kidney cells use it in respiration, so less in the renal vein.
  4. Carbon dioxide: kidney cells make it in respiration, so more in the renal vein.
  5. Four marks: I give differences with reasons.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Blood in the renal vein contains less urea than blood in the renal artery, because urea is filtered out of the blood and excreted in the urine. Renal vein blood also contains less oxygen and less glucose, because kidney cells use them in respiration. It contains more carbon dioxide, because carbon dioxide is produced by respiration in the kidney cells.

How the marks are earned

Marks for: less glucose or oxygen in the renal vein; because they are used in respiration; less urea in the renal vein; because urea is excreted in urine; more carbon dioxide in the renal vein; because respiration produces it. Any four.
