Functions and food sources of named nutrients and the deficiency diseases scurvy, anaemia, vitamin A blindness and kwashiorkor.

Mostly recall with a one-step link. A small set of nutrient-disease pairs. No calculation. Some contexts are unfamiliar (pellagra, pinworms, sailors) but the answer is still recall. Stands mainly alone.

Knowledge card

Link a nutrient to its function, source and deficiency disease

Written

Link each nutrient to its job and to the deficiency disease its lack causes. Deficiency diseases are not infectious.

4

The method

  1. Find the nutrient

    Which one is named?

  2. Give its job

    What it does in the body.

  3. Name the disease

    Or a symptom of the lack.

  4. Explain a trend

    Link it to diet, education or food aid.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Mixed diseases

    Scurvy is vitamin C; anaemia is iron.

  • ✗
    "Prevents colds"

    Vitamin C makes connective tissue.

  • ✗
    Protein gives energy

    Growth and repair is its job.

  • ✗
    Infectious

    Deficiency diseases cannot spread.

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Explain why vitamin C is needed. Two marks: a job and what happens without it.
  2. Job: it is needed to make connective tissue that holds cells together, for example in gums and skin.
  3. Without it: scurvy, with bleeding gums.
  4. Write both ideas as linked sentences.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Vitamin C is needed to make connective tissue, which holds cells and tissues together, for example in the gums and skin. A lack of vitamin C causes scurvy, where the gums bleed.

How the marks are earned

One mark for the connective tissue role (holding cells together or keeping the gums strong) and one for naming scurvy or a symptom like bleeding gums. Either two of the three ideas gives full marks.
