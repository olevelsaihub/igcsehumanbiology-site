Explain causes of obesity, its health risks and dietary advice

Written

Obesity happens when energy in is more than energy used over time; the extra is stored as fat. It raises the risk of several diseases.

Do not lose marks here

Worked explanation

  1. Three marks, so three ideas.
  2. Intake side: eating too much food, especially high in fat or sugar.
  3. Output side: too little exercise, so less energy is used.
  4. Result: the extra energy is stored as fat in the body.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Obesity is caused by eating too much food, especially food high in fat and sugar, and by doing too little exercise. More energy is taken in than is used, so the extra energy is stored as fat under the skin and around organs.

How the marks are earned

One mark for eating too much, one for too little exercise, and one for the extra being stored as fat.
