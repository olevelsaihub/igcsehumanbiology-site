4

The method

  1. Sort left and right

    Find the labels, or take the left side as your right.

  2. Find the chamber

    Note which chamber the label touches.

  3. Use the rule

    Arteries leave ventricles; veins enter atria.

  4. Properties

    Think where the blood has just been: lungs or body.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong side

    Left is the viewer's right.

  • ✗
    Pulmonary vein and artery

    The vein enters the left atrium; the artery leaves the right ventricle.

  • ✗
    Vague names

    Write "pulmonary artery", not just "artery".

Worked explanation

  1. The diagram marks 'right' under chambers Q and P and 'left' under R and S.
  2. Q and R are the top chambers, so they are atria. P and S are the bottom chambers, so they are ventricles.
  3. Vessel M is the tube on the right side. It goes down to the valves at the top of the right ventricle P.
  4. A vessel leaving the right ventricle carries blood to the lungs. That is the pulmonary artery.
  5. So the answer is B, pulmonary artery. The aorta would leave the left ventricle (vessel N).
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

B (pulmonary artery)

How the marks are earned

One mark for B. A is wrong because the aorta leaves the left ventricle. C is wrong because the pulmonary vein enters the left atrium. D is wrong because the vena cava enters the right atrium.
