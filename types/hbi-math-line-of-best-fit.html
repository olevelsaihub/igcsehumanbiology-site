Draw a line or curve of best fit through plotted points

Points are already plotted and a single straight line or smooth curve showing the trend is requested.

Type 2 Maths skills HBI-MATH-LINE-OF-BEST-FIT

★★★☆☆ · 6 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Easy.

Students must judge straight or curved and spot anomalies. Understanding that a best-fit line shows a trend. No calculation. Very common task. Stands alone after plotting.

4

The method

  1. Judge

    Is the pattern straight or curved?

  2. Spot

    Find any anomalous point and leave it out.

  3. Draw

    One thin smooth line, points both sides.

  4. Stop

    End at the first and last points.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Dot to dot

    Joined points score nothing.

  • ✗
    Forcing it

    Do not bend the line to an anomaly.

  • ✗
    Wrong shape

    Curved data need a curve.

Worked explanation

  1. Look at the plotted pH values: 6.8, 6.4, 6.0, 5.8, 5.6, 5.2, 5.0, 4.8, 4.6, 4.6 from 1 to 10 minutes.
  2. The pH falls steadily, then the last two points are the same, so the line flattens a little at the end.
  3. None of the points is far from the pattern, so there is no anomaly to leave out.
  4. Draw one thin smooth line from the 1-minute point to the 10-minute point, with the points balanced on both sides.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

One single, thin, smooth line drawn from 1 minute to 10 minutes that follows the falling pH, passing among the points with roughly equal numbers above and below it and flattening slightly at the end. It does not join the points dot to dot.

How the marks are earned

One mark for a single line that fits the trend of the points. A dot-to-dot line, or a line that ignores the overall pattern, does not get the mark.
