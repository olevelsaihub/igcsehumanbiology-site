Glomerulus

Job

Bowman's capsule

Shape
The cup that surrounds the glomerulus.

Proximal convoluted tubule

Job

Shape

Collecting duct

Job

4

The method

  1. Follow the path

    Glomerulus, capsule, proximal tubule, loop, distal tubule, collecting duct.

  2. Use the shape

    A cup round a knot; a long U; a tube receiving many tubules.

  3. Whole system

    Ureter: kidney to bladder. Urethra: bladder out.

  4. Tick tables

    One tick per column.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Ureter and urethra

    Ureter joins the kidney; urethra leaves the bladder.

  • ✗
    Glomerulus or capsule

    Knot of capillaries versus the cup round it.

  • ✗
    Loop before proximal

    The proximal tubule comes first.

Worked explanation

  1. M points to the knot of capillaries in the cup: that is the glomerulus.
  2. L points to the cup wall around the knot: that is the Bowman's capsule.
  3. N points to the straight branching tube on the right, which receives fluid from the tubule: that is the collecting duct.
  4. O points to the long hairpin loop going down: that is the loop of Henle.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

L: Bowman's capsule. M: glomerulus. N: collecting duct. O: loop of Henle.

How the marks are earned

One mark for each correct name. Swapping L and M loses both marks.
