Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Human Biology · 4HB1

Interpret a menstrual-cycle graph of hormones or lining thickness

Graphs of hormone concentration or uterine lining width across a 28-day cycle.

Type 12 Reproduction and heredity HBI-INTERPRET-MENSTRUAL-CYCLE-GRAPH

★☆☆☆☆ · 2 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

Links a graph trend to a hormone and then to fertilisation or implantation, and predicts changes. Hormone interactions over time must be understood, not just recalled. Reading values and finding a difference or day range. Graphs differ between papers in scale and in which lines are shown. Combines graph skills with hormone and pregnancy knowledge.

The method

  1. Axes and key

    Which line is which, and the units.

  2. Read the days

    Use a ruler up from the x-axis.

  3. Match the hormone

    Say what it does to the lining.

  4. Give the purpose

    A thick lining is ready for implantation.

  5. If fertilised

    Describe each line separately.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Wrong y-axis

    Read lining values off the lining scale.

  • ✗
    Shape only

    Say why the change matters.

  • ✗
    Oestrogen after ovulation

    Progesterone is rising.

  • ✗
    Rising for ever

    If fertilised, both lines level off.

Worked explanation

  1. Part (i) asked for the change in lining width between day 6 and day 14. From the graph the width goes from about 0.6 mm to about 4.25 mm, an increase of about 3.6 mm.
  2. So 'this change' is an increase: the lining gets thicker before ovulation.
  3. Three marks: what happens, when it matters and why it matters.
  4. It matters only if the egg is fertilised.
  5. A thick lining lets the embryo implant and gives it blood vessels for food and oxygen.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

The lining becomes thicker between day 6 and day 14. This is important in case the egg is fertilised after ovulation. The thick lining, with a good blood supply, allows the embryo to implant in the uterus wall and be supplied with nutrients and oxygen.

How the marks are earned

One mark for saying the lining increases in thickness, one for linking it to fertilisation happening, and one for saying it lets implantation occur. A description of the line with no link to implantation would earn at most one mark.

Calculation check

  • 4.25-0.625 = 3.625
