Knowledge card

Draw or label the parts of a synovial joint

Written

A synovial joint has cartilage on both bone ends, synovial fluid in the gap, a synovial membrane lining it and a capsule with ligaments outside.

1

4 parts to find

Name each part by what it does.

  1. Cartilage
  2. Synovial fluid
  3. Synovial membrane
  4. Capsule and ligaments

Cartilage

Draw it
On the ball and lining the socket.

Exam tipOn both bone ends, not one.

Synovial fluid

Draw it
Fill the gap between the cartilage layers.

Synovial membrane

Draw it
A thin lining inside the capsule.

Exam tipThe membrane makes the fluid; it is not the fluid.

Capsule and ligaments

Draw it
Around the joint, joining the two bones.

Exam tipA tendon is not the capsule.

4

The method

  1. Bones

    Draw two bone ends with a small gap.

  2. Cartilage

    Cover both bone ends and label.

  3. Fluid

    Fill the gap and label.

  4. Lining and capsule

    Add the membrane, capsule and ligaments.

  5. Tidy

    Sharp pencil, ruled lines that touch the parts.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    One bone end

    Cartilage covers both ends.

  • ✗
    Fluid and membrane

    Fluid fills the gap; membrane lines it.

  • ✗
    Tendon as capsule

    Use capsule or ligament.

  • ✗
    Wrong shape

    Draw a ball and socket if that is the joint.

Worked explanation

  1. The synovial joint shown is at the top of the humerus, where it meets the shoulder. That is a ball and socket joint.
  2. So I draw the round head of the humerus fitting into a cup (the socket of the scapula), and label both.
  3. I draw a layer of cartilage over the ball and inside the socket, and label it.
  4. I fill the space between them with synovial fluid and label it.
  5. I draw the synovial membrane lining the joint, and a fibrous capsule with ligaments outside it. I label both.
  6. I keep lines clear and label lines straight, for the overall quality mark.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

A clear labelled drawing showing: the rounded head of the humerus (ball) fitting into the socket of the scapula, labelled as bones; a layer of cartilage covering the ball and lining the socket, labelled 'cartilage'; the space between them filled and labelled 'synovial fluid'; a thin layer lining the joint labelled 'synovial membrane'; and a tough outer layer joining the two bones labelled 'capsule / ligament'. Outlines are smooth and label lines are ruled and touch the parts.

How the marks are earned

One mark each for: the ball and socket bones drawn and labelled; capsule or ligament labelled; cartilage labelled; synovial fluid labelled; synovial membrane labelled; a clear overall diagram.
