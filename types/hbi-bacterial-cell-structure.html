Identify bacterial cell structures and compare with human cells

Labelling bacterial cells and comparing their parts and functions with human cells such as skin cells or sperm.

Type 2 Disease HBI-BACTERIAL-CELL-STRUCTURE

★★★★☆ · 7 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Easy.

Direct recall and comparison with no chain of reasoning. Cell structures are concrete and well known. No mathematics. Diagrams and photographs vary and sometimes compare with sperm cells. Uses cell biology only.

The method

  1. Read the task

    Similarities or differences, and how many?

  2. Name the structure

    Choose one found in only one cell.

  3. Give the direction

    Say which cell has it.

  4. Label carefully

    Follow the line to the exact structure.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    No DNA

    Bacteria have DNA, just not in a nucleus.

  • ✗
    No membrane

    Both cells have a cell membrane.

  • ✗
    Flagellum names

    Not cilia or tail.

  • ✗
    Wall or membrane

    The outer layer is the wall.

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. 'Give two differences', 2 marks: two different structures, each in only one cell.
  2. Look at the bacterium: thick outer wall, loose DNA, a long flagellum. Look at the skin cell: a clear nucleus, no wall.
  3. Difference 1: the bacterium has a cell wall; the skin cell does not.
  4. Difference 2: the bacterium has no nucleus; the skin cell does.
  5. Make each answer a full sentence that names both cells.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

1 The bacterial cell has a cell wall, but the human skin cell does not.

2 The bacterial cell has no nucleus (its DNA is free in the cytoplasm), but the human skin cell has a nucleus.

How the marks are earned

One mark for each difference that names a structure present in one cell only: cell wall, nucleus, plasmid, flagellum, pili, mitochondria or slime capsule. A structure both cells have, such as the membrane, scores nothing.
