Relate sperm structures to their roles in fertilisation

Labelling sperm parts and explaining the roles of the tail, mitochondria-packed midpiece, acrosome and haploid nucleus.

Knowledge card

Relate sperm structures to their roles in fertilisation

Written

Each sperm part links to fertilisation: the acrosome releases enzymes, the mid-piece releases energy for the tail, and the nucleus fuses with the egg.

The method

  1. Name the part

    Tip of head: acrosome. Middle: mid-piece.

  2. Build a chain

    Feature, what it does, why it helps.

  3. Say respiration

    Mitochondria release energy for swimming.

  4. End at the egg

    Link to reaching or entering the egg.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Makes energy

    Write releases energy by respiration.

  • ✗
    Contains enzymes

    Say they are released and digest the jelly coat.

  • ✗
    Cell wall

    The egg has a jelly coat, not a wall.

  • ✗
    Stopping at movement

    Add: to reach the egg.

Worked explanation

  1. Part C is the small cap at the very tip of the sperm head, so it is the acrosome.
  2. The question says 'describe the function' and gives 3 marks, so I need three linked ideas.
  3. Idea 1: the acrosome releases enzymes when the sperm reaches the egg.
  4. Idea 2: the enzymes digest the jelly coat, the outer layer of the egg.
  5. Idea 3: this makes a path so the sperm nucleus can get into the egg, and fertilisation can happen.
  6. I must not just write 'contains enzymes', because that does not describe a function.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

When the sperm reaches the egg, the acrosome releases digestive enzymes. These enzymes break down the jelly coat around the outside of the egg. This lets the sperm nucleus enter the egg so that it can fuse with the egg nucleus in fertilisation.

How the marks are earned

Any three from: the enzymes are released; they digest the egg's outer layer or jelly coat; this lets the sperm nucleus get in; a link to fertilisation. Saying the acrosome merely contains or produces enzymes is ignored, and so is a reference to the egg cell membrane.
