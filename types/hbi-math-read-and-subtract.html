Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Human Biology · 4HB1

Read two values from a graph, chart or table and find the difference

Two points, bars, peaks and troughs, or ends of a range are located and the gap between them is the answer.

Type 5 Maths skills HBI-MATH-READ-AND-SUBTRACT

★★★★★ · 12 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Easy.

Students must find the named points. Little biology is needed. Scale reading and one subtraction. Graph reading is routine. Combines graph reading with subtraction.

The method

  1. Scale

    Value of one small square on each axis.

  2. Points

    Find the two the question names.

  3. Read

    Write down both values.

  4. Subtract

    Larger minus smaller, then the unit.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Small square as 1

    It may be worth 2 or 5.

  • ✗
    Wrong line or bar

    Check which is which.

  • ✗
    One reading only

    The answer is the difference.

Worked explanation

  1. The y-axis goes up in 20s for each big square, and each big square has 10 small squares, so one small square is 2 beats per minute.
  2. The resting heart rate is the flat line at the start: 72 beats per minute.
  3. The maximum is the flat top between about 14 and 20 minutes: 156 beats per minute.
  4. Difference = 156 - 72 = 84 beats per minute.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Maximum = 156 beats per minute; resting = 72 beats per minute. Difference = 156 - 72 = 84 beats per minute.

How the marks are earned

One mark for the two correct readings, 156 and 72, used in a subtraction. One mark for 84. If a reading is wrong but subtracted correctly, one mark can still be given.

Calculation check

  • 156-72 = 84
