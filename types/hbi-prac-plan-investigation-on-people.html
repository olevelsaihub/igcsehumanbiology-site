Plan an investigation measuring a response or treatment in people

Pulse, reaction time, pupil size, catching, hearing, sweating or urine volume in volunteers, and treated versus untreated group trials of drugs or diets.

Type 11 Investigations HBI-PRAC-PLAN-INVESTIGATION-ON-PEOPLE

★★★★★ · 13 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Difficult.

A multi-step plan with a logical order and a recovery step. Needs knowledge of pulse, reflexes or drug action, plus fair-test ideas. No calculation is required by the scheme. Wide range of contexts: stereoscopic vision, urine, statins, vitamin B. Combines variables, controls, measurement method, repeats and comparison.

Worked explanation

  1. The graph shows pulse rate against time spent exercising, from 0 to 100 seconds.
  2. So the independent variable is time exercising and the dependent variable is pulse rate.
  3. Start with a resting pulse, fingers on the wrist.
  4. Exercise for a set time, then count the pulse straight away.
  5. Rest until the pulse is back to resting, then exercise for a longer time.
  6. Add controls (same person, same exercise) and repeats with a mean.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

The student sits still for 5 minutes and then measures the resting pulse by placing two fingers on the wrist and counting the beats for 15 seconds, then multiplying by 4. The student then does step-ups for 20 seconds and measures the pulse again immediately after stopping. The student rests until the pulse returns to the resting rate. This is repeated with exercise times of 40, 60, 80 and 100 seconds. The same person does the same exercise at the same speed each time. Each exercise time is repeated three times and a mean pulse rate is calculated.

How the marks are earned

Marks for: a resting pulse before exercise; fingers on the wrist or neck (or a monitor); doing the exercise; recording pulse afterwards; waiting until pulse is back to normal; repeating; naming a control variable such as same person or same exercise intensity.
