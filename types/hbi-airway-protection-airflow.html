Explain how airway features keep air clean and flowing

Written

Mucus traps dust and pathogens; cilia sweep it away; cartilage rings keep the airways open. Smoke damages cilia, so infection risk rises.

The method

  1. Feature and job

    Mucus traps, cilia sweep, cartilage holds open.

  2. Result

    Air flows freely; fewer pathogens arrive.

  3. Damage chain

    Cilia stop, mucus builds up, airways narrow, infection.

  4. Nose or mouth

    Nose warms, moistens and cleans.

Do not lose marks here

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. The diagram shows more mucus and damaged cilia in chronic bronchitis.
  2. Healthy cilia sweep mucus away. Damaged cilia cannot do this, so mucus builds up and blocks the airways.
  3. Mucus contains trapped bacteria. If it is not removed, the bacteria stay in the lungs.
  4. So the person has a higher risk of lung infections and coughs a lot.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

The damaged cilia cannot sweep the mucus up and away, so mucus builds up and blocks the airways, and bacteria trapped in it are not removed. This increases the risk of lung infections and causes coughing.

How the marks are earned

One mark for mucus not being removed, building up or blocking the airways, or bacteria not being removed. One mark for the result: a higher risk of respiratory infection or coughing.
