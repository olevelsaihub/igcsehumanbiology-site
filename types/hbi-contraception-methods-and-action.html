Human Biology / Name contraceptive methods, explain how they work and compare them

Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Human Biology · 4HB1

Name contraceptive methods, explain how they work and compare them

Barrier, hormonal, natural, IUD and surgical methods: naming examples, explaining the mechanism, limits such as infection protection, and weighing two methods with data.

Type 13 Reproduction and heredity HBI-CONTRACEPTION-METHODS-AND-ACTION

★★★☆☆ · 6 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

Ranges from naming to explaining mechanisms and evaluating two methods with a judgement. Mechanisms are simple once the reproductive system is known. Only comparing two percentages. Diagrams of a vasectomy or data tables add some new context. Links contraception with anatomy, hormones and disease transmission.

Worked explanation

  1. Three rows, three marks: one example for each group.
  2. Barrier: something that physically stops sperm; I choose a condom.
  3. Natural: no device or drug; I choose the rhythm method (avoiding sex near ovulation).
  4. Hormonal: a drug containing hormones; I choose the contraceptive pill.
  5. I check each example is in the right row.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

barrier: condom

natural: rhythm method

hormonal: contraceptive pill (oestrogen and progesterone pill)

How the marks are earned

One mark per row: a barrier device such as condom, cap, diaphragm or femidom; the rhythm method (withdrawal also accepted); a pill containing progesterone, with or without oestrogen.
