Identify alimentary canal organs and describe what each does

Naming, labelling or locating digestive organs from a diagram or feature, and describing the job of the mouth, pancreas, small or large intestine.

Knowledge card

Identify alimentary canal organs and describe what each does

Written

Food passes through the gut in order: mouth, oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, anus. The liver and pancreas join by ducts.

Say the role of each organ.

  1. Chewing; amylase
  2. Acid and protease
  3. Finishes digestion; absorbs
  4. Absorbs water

Chewing; amylase

Write it as
Teeth chew food; saliva amylase digests starch to maltose.

Acid and protease

Write it as
Hydrochloric acid (pH 2) and pepsin start protein digestion.

Exam tipProtein digestion does not start in the mouth.

Finishes digestion; absorbs

Write it as
Digestion is finished and products are absorbed through villi.

Absorbs water

Write it as
Absorbs most of the remaining water.

Exam tipWithout it, dehydration.

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Start at the mouth. X is the narrow tube going down through the chest: the oesophagus.
  2. Y is the bag on the left side below the chest that the oesophagus enters: the stomach.
  3. Z points to the many tight coils in the centre of the abdomen: the small intestine.
  4. W points to the wider tube running round the outside of the coils: the large intestine.
  5. Check the order: oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

W: large intestine (colon)

X: oesophagus

Y: stomach

Z: small intestine (ileum)

How the marks are earned

One mark for each correct name. Colon is allowed for W and ileum for Z.
