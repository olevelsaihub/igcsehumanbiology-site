Describe the products and purposes of mitosis

What mitosis produces, drawing daughter cells or nuclei, cell cycle growth and division phases, uses of mitosis and contrast with meiosis.

Type 13 Cells and tissues HBI-DESCRIBE-MITOSIS-PRODUCTS-AND-USES

★★★★☆ · 7 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

Some parts need a comparison with meiosis or reading a graph. Needs diploid, haploid and genetic identity. Counting chromosomes in diagrams. Cell-cycle diagrams and DNA-mass graphs vary. Links mitosis, DNA replication and meiosis.

The method

  1. Three facts

    Two cells, identical, diploid.

  2. Drawings

    Two cells, each with the parent's chromosomes.

  3. Uses

    Growth or repair, in context.

  4. Compare

    Cell number and chromosome number.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Four cells

    Mitosis makes two.

  • ✗
    Half the chromosomes

    That is meiosis.

  • ✗
    Similar

    Write genetically identical.

  • ✗
    Gametes

    Not a use of mitosis in humans.

Worked explanation

  1. The question asks what is produced by mitosis. 2 marks, so I need two features.
  2. Feature 1: two cells, and they are genetically identical to the parent cell.
  3. Feature 2: they are diploid, with the full set of chromosome pairs (46 in humans).
  4. I write both in one clear sentence each.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Mitosis produces two daughter cells that are genetically identical to each other and to the parent cell. Each daughter cell is diploid, with the same number of chromosomes as the parent (46 in a human body cell).

How the marks are earned

One mark for two genetically identical cells. One mark for diploid (2n).
