Human Biology / Calculate a percentage change or percentage difference

Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Human Biology · 4HB1

Calculate a percentage change or percentage difference

Two values of one quantity, from text, a table or a graph, compared as a percentage of a stated starting or reference value.

Type 12 Maths skills HBI-MATH-PERCENTAGE-CHANGE

★★★★★ · 10 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

Students must choose which value is the original one. The idea of change relative to a start value is simple but often confused with percentage of a total. Two operations plus rounding, and sometimes unit or million conversions. Contexts vary (deaths, urine volume, blood flow) but the method is always the same. Often needs a value read from a graph or bar chart first.

The method

  1. Two values

    Write both down with units.

  2. Pick the original

    The starting value, or after "compared with".

  3. Subtract

    New minus original.

  4. Divide and × 100

    Change ÷ original × 100; say increase or decrease.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    New value on the bottom

    Always divide by the original.

  • ✗
    Mixed units

    1.4 million is 1 400 000.

  • ✗
    Early rounding

    Keep digits until the last step.

Worked explanation

  1. The question compares heart disease deaths in 2000 with 2016. 2000 comes first, so 2000 is the original value.
  2. The text gives 2000: 7.0 million deaths.
  3. The bar chart is for 2016. The heart disease bar ends halfway between 9 and 10, so it is 9.5 million.
  4. Change = 9.5 - 7.0 = 2.5 million. It went up, so this is an increase.
  5. Divide the change by the original value: 2.5 / 7.0 = 0.357...
  6. Multiply by 100: 35.7...%. Round to a whole number: 36%.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Deaths in 2016 = 9.5 million (from the bar chart). Change = 9.5 - 7.0 = 2.5 million. Percentage change = 2.5 / 7.0 x 100 = 35.7% = 36% (increase).

How the marks are earned

One mark for the subtraction 9.5 - 7.0 giving 2.5 million. One mark for dividing 2.5 by the 2000 value of 7.0 and multiplying by 100. One mark for the final answer of 36%, or 35.7%. A correct final answer on its own gets all three marks. Dividing by 9.5 does not earn the method mark.

Calculation check

  • 9.5-7.0 = 2.5
  • (9.5-7.0) ÷ 7.0 × 100 = 35.714285714285715
