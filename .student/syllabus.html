Syllabus

Every official statement and bullet, with the question types that cover it.

Source: Pearson Edexcel International GCSE Human Biology specification, Issue 2 (September 2024). Statement codes and wording are official; the specification has no subtopic level, so statements sit directly under the sections.

1. Cells and tissues

1.1 recognise cell structures as seen with a light microscope and electron microscope (TEM images only), including nucleus, chromosomes, cell membrane, mitochondria, endoplasmic reticulum and ribosomes

21 past-paper parts assess this directly (7 main, 14 regional).

Question types: Locate DNA and chromosomes in a human cell · Compare light and electron microscopes and use their parts · Draw or label a human cell as seen under the microscope · Prepare cheek-cell or blood slides for a light microscope

  • nucleus
  • chromosomes
  • cell membrane
  • mitochondria
  • endoplasmic reticulum
  • ribosomes.

19 past-paper parts assess this directly (11 main, 8 regional).

Question types: Locate DNA and chromosomes in a human cell · Match human cell structures to their functions · Relate sperm structures to their roles in fertilisation

  • two strands coiled to form a double helix
  • containing nucleotides
  • strands linked by complementary bases
  • complementary bases linked by hydrogen bonds.

20 past-paper parts assess this directly (16 main, 4 regional).

Question types: Name the parts of a nucleotide and the DNA backbone · Compare DNA with RNA · Describe DNA's double helix, base pairing and replication

3 past-paper parts assess this directly (2 main, 1 regional).

  • 3 bases coding for one amino acid.
