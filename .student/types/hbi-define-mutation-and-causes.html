Define a gene mutation and name its causes

Written

A mutation is a change in the sequence of bases in DNA. Mutagens such as ultraviolet light and X-rays raise the rate of mutation.

Complete the definition.

  1. The sequence of bases
  2. In the DNA
  3. Swap, loss or gain of a base

The sequence of bases

Write it as
A change in the order of bases.

Exam tipNot "a change in a cell".

In the DNA

Write it as
In a molecule of DNA, or in a gene.

Swap, loss or gain of a base

Meaning

The method

  1. Start

    A change in the order of bases.

  2. Place it

    In the DNA, or a gene.

  3. Effect

    A different protein may be made.

  4. Cause

    Name a mutagen: UV light, X-rays.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Change in a cell

    Mention bases and DNA.

  • ✗
    Always harmful

    Some have no effect.

  • ✗
    Amino acids in DNA

    DNA has bases.

  • ✗
    Vague cause

    Name ultraviolet light or X-rays.

Worked explanation

  1. The question asks what is meant by mutation. There are 2 marks.
  2. Mark 1: what changes. The order of bases (the base sequence) changes.
  3. Mark 2: where. The change is in the DNA.
  4. I can name a type, such as a substitution, as an example, but the two ideas above are what count.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

A mutation is a change in the sequence of bases in a molecule of DNA, for example when one base is replaced by a different base.

How the marks are earned

One mark for a change in the base sequence (nucleotide or codon sequence is also accepted). One mark for saying the change is in DNA.
