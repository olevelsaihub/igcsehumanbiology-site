The method

  1. Main effect

    Start with what the condition does.

  2. Organs

    Name where the mucus builds up.

  3. Consequence

    One effect for each organ.

  4. No repeats

    The same effect twice scores once.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Lungs only

    A 4-mark answer needs gut and fertility too.

  • ✗
    Infection

    Cystic fibrosis cannot be caught.

  • ✗
    Missing fingers

    Polydactyly is extra digits.

Worked explanation

  1. Four marks: I need four different effects.
  2. Root cause: thick, sticky mucus builds up on membranes.
  3. Lungs: mucus blocks airways, causing coughing and difficulty breathing, and less oxygen is absorbed.
  4. With less oxygen there is less aerobic respiration and so less energy.
  5. Gut and pancreas: digestion and absorption of nutrients is reduced.
  6. Sperm ducts: blocked in males, causing infertility.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Thick, sticky mucus builds up on membranes in the lungs, pancreas, small intestine and sperm ducts. In the lungs this causes coughing and difficulty breathing, and less oxygen is absorbed by gas exchange. So cells carry out less aerobic respiration and the person has less energy. In the gut, less food is digested and fewer nutrients are absorbed. Males may also be infertile because the sperm ducts are blocked.

How the marks are earned

Any four of: mucus build-up; named organs where it collects; coughing, breathing difficulty or less gas exchange; less respiration or energy; less digestion or absorption; male infertility.
