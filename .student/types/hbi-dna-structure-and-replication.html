Describe DNA's double helix, base pairing and replication

Two strands linked by complementary bases and hydrogen bonds, deducing bases or proportions, and copying DNA with DNA polymerase before division.

Type 11 Cells and tissues HBI-DNA-STRUCTURE-AND-REPLICATION

★★★★☆ · 7 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

Order replication steps and use the pairing rule on data. Molecular structure and copying are abstract ideas. Simple percentage reasoning with base data. Graphs of DNA mass and base tables appear in new forms. Links DNA structure to mitosis.

DNA is a double helix of two strands joined by hydrogen bonds: A with T, C with G. In replication, free nucleotides pair with each old strand.

Strands separate

Write it as
The helix unwinds and the two strands separate.

Free nucleotides pair

Write it as
Free nucleotides pair with each old strand by complementary base pairing.

DNA polymerase joins

Write it as
The enzyme DNA polymerase joins them into a new strand.

Exam tipIt joins nucleotides; it does not unzip DNA.

The method

  1. List facts

    Strands, helix, nucleotides, four bases.

  2. Name the pairs

    A with T, C with G.

  3. Name the bond

    Hydrogen bonds.

  4. Order replication

    Unwind, pair, join, two molecules.

Worked explanation

  1. The question asks me to describe the structure of DNA for 4 marks. I need at least four separate facts.
  2. Fact 1: DNA has two strands twisted into a double helix.
  3. Fact 2: each strand is a chain of nucleotides.
  4. Fact 3: there are four bases, A, T, C and G.
  5. Fact 4: the bases pair in a complementary way, A with T and C with G.
  6. Fact 5: the paired bases are held together by hydrogen bonds.
  7. Five facts for four marks gives me a safety margin.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

DNA is made of two strands that are coiled to form a double helix. Each strand is a chain of nucleotides. There are four different bases: adenine, thymine, cytosine and guanine. The bases pair in a complementary way: adenine always pairs with thymine and cytosine always pairs with guanine. The base pairs are held together by hydrogen bonds, which link the two strands.

How the marks are earned

Any four from: the double helix or two strands; being built from nucleotides; four bases; hydrogen bonds between bases; complementary pairing A-T and C-G. The model answer gives all five.
