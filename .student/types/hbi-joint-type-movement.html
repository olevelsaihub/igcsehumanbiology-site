Knowledge card

Name joint types and compare their movement

Written

A joint is where two bones meet. The elbow and knee are hinge joints (one plane). The shoulder and hip are ball and socket joints (three planes and rotation).

The method

  1. Find the joint

    Shoulder or hip, elbow or knee.

  2. Name the type

    Write hinge or ball and socket.

  3. Planes

    Say how many planes, and whether it rotates.

  4. Both sides

    A comparison describes both joints.

Do not lose marks here

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Joint A is a ball and socket joint (like the hip). Joint B is a hinge joint (like the knee).
  2. The question asks me to compare their range of movement, so I must say something about each one.
  3. A ball and socket joint can move in three planes and can rotate.
  4. A hinge joint can move in one plane only: backwards and forwards.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Joint A, a ball and socket joint, has a wide range of movement: it can move in all three planes and can also rotate. Joint B, a hinge joint, has a smaller range: it bends and straightens in a single plane only, like a door.

How the marks are earned

One mark for joint A moving in three planes or rotating. One mark for joint B moving in one plane only (backwards and forwards). A description in words is accepted.
