BMI = mass in kg ÷ (height in m)². Square the height first, then use the paper's table to name the weight category.

The calculation

BMI = mass ÷ height²

  • Height × 2 is not height squared.
  • Mass ÷ height², never the other way up.

The method

  1. Metres

    Change the height to metres.

  2. Square

    Height × height.

  3. Divide

    Mass ÷ height², round to 1 d.p.

  4. Classify

    Find the band in the given table.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Doubling

    Squared means × itself, not × 2.

  • ✗
    Boundary values

    30.0 is obese; check the table's edges.

  • ✗
    No working

Worked explanation

  1. Mass is 120 kg and height is 1.95 m. Both are in the right units.
  2. Square the height: 1.95 × 1.95 = 3.8025.
  3. Divide the mass by this: 120 ÷ 3.8025 = 31.558...
  4. Round to one decimal place: 31.6.
  5. Sense check: 31.6 sits in the obese band (30.0-39.9), which fits a heavy person.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

BMI = 120 ÷ 1.95² = 120 ÷ 3.8025 = 31.6

How the marks are earned

One mark for the working (120 divided by 1.95 squared) and one for the answer 31.6. An unrounded 31.56 or 31.5581, or 32, is also accepted.

Calculation check

  • round(1.95 × × 2, 4) = 3.8025
  • round(120 ÷ 1.95 × × 2, 1) = 31.6
  • round(120 ÷ 1.95 × × 2, 4) = 31.5582
