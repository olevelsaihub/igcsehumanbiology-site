Convert units and write numbers in standard form or significant figures

Calculation

Standard form is A × 10ⁿ with A from 1 to under 10. For significant figures, count from the first non-zero digit and keep the size with zeros.

The method

  1. Read the ask

    Which form or unit?

  2. Point

    After the first non-zero digit.

  3. Power

    Count the places; negative for numbers under 1.

  4. Round

    Look at the next digit; fill with zeros.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    A out of range

    A must be from 1 to under 10.

  • ✗
    Power off by one

    Count the places again.

  • ✗
    Dropping zeros

    3 320 000, not 332.

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. The number is 600 239.
  2. Put the decimal point after the first digit: 6.00239.
  3. Count the places from 6.00239 back to 600 239: the point moves 5 places, so the power is 5.
  4. 600 239 = 6.00239 x 10^5.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

6.00239 x 10^5

How the marks are earned

One mark for the digits 6.00239 (6.0024 is accepted). One mark for x 10^5.

Calculation check

  • 6.00239 × 10 × × 5 = 600239.0
