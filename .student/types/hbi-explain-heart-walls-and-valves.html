The method

  1. Compare

    Say which chamber has the thicker wall.

  2. Muscle and force

    More muscle gives a stronger contraction.

  3. Where it pumps

    Whole body, lungs or ventricles.

  4. Pressure needed

    High for the body, lower for the lungs.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    "Holds more blood"

    Link thickness to muscle and pressure.

  • ✗
    "Stronger" alone

    Say stronger because of more muscle.

  • ✗
    Valves "push"

    Valves only stop backflow.

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. From the front view, X is the right ventricle and Y is the left ventricle.
  2. Fact: Y has the thicker wall.
  3. Why? Y pumps blood into the aorta.
  4. The aorta takes blood to all the organs of the whole body, which is far.
  5. That needs a high pressure.
  6. A thicker wall has more muscle, so it contracts more strongly. X only pumps to the lungs, so it needs less force.
  7. I write each idea as a separate point.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Chamber Y (the left ventricle) has a thicker wall than X (the right ventricle). Y pumps blood into the aorta, which carries it to all the organs and tissues of the whole body. This needs a high pressure. The thicker wall contains more muscle, so it contracts more strongly. X only pumps blood a short distance to the lungs, so it needs less muscle and a lower pressure.

How the marks are earned

Marks go for: Y being thicker; blood going into the aorta; blood going to the organs or whole body; the need for high pressure; and more muscle giving a stronger contraction. The reverse argument about X is accepted for each point.
