Worked explanation

  1. I find the 1.60% carbon dioxide column: tidal volume 700 cm3, rate 15 breaths per minute.
  2. Volume breathed in per minute = 700 x 15 = 10 500 cm3.
  3. The air is 20% oxygen: 10 500 x 20 / 100 = 2100 cm3 of oxygen breathed in per minute.
  4. The body uses 20% of this oxygen: 2100 x 20 / 100 = 420 cm3.
  5. The answer line is in cm3, so no conversion is needed.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Volume of air per minute = 700 x 15 = 10 500 cm3. Oxygen in this air = 10 500 x 20/100 = 2100 cm3. Oxygen used = 2100 x 20/100 = 420 cm3 per minute.

How the marks are earned

One mark for finding 20% of the per-minute air volume, one for finding 20% of that oxygen volume, and one for the final 420 cm3. A wrong per-minute volume can still earn the later marks if the method is right.

Calculation check

  • 700 × 15 = 10500
  • 700 × 15 × 20 ÷ 100 = 2100.0
  • 700 × 15 × 20 ÷ 100 × 20 ÷ 100 = 420.0
