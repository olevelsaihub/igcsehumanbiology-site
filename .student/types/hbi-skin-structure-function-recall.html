Identify skin structures and state what each does

Written

The skin has three layers: epidermis, dermis and a fat layer. Give each structure a specific job, such as "insulates, reducing heat loss".

4 jobs to find

Say what each part does.

  1. Insulates
  2. Absorbs UV light
  3. Releases sweat
  4. Senses touch and pain

Insulates

Write it as
Insulates the body, reducing heat loss.

Exam tip"Keeps the body warm" is too vague.

Absorbs UV light

Write it as
Melanin absorbs ultraviolet light, lowering the risk of skin cancer.

Exam tipSay UV, not just "the sun".

Releases sweat

Write it as
Sweat glands release sweat to cool the body.

Senses touch and pain

Write it as
Receptors in the dermis detect touch, pain and temperature.

4

The method

  1. Find the layer

    Thin outer: epidermis. Thick middle: dermis. Bottom: fat.

  2. Name exactly

    Sweat gland, hair follicle, sebaceous gland, arteriole.

  3. Specific job

    Write "insulates, reducing heat loss".

  4. Infection

    Broken skin lets pathogens into the body or blood.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    "Keeps warm"

    Say insulates and reduces heat loss.

  • ✗
    Melanin "blocks the sun"

    It absorbs UV light.

  • ✗
    Sweat and sebaceous glands

    Check which one the diagram shows.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. The question asks about the layer of fat and body temperature.
  2. Fat is a poor conductor of heat. So it is an insulator.
  3. An insulating layer reduces the heat energy lost from the body to the surroundings.
  4. I avoid writing only 'keeps the body warm' because that is too vague.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

The layer of fat acts as an insulating layer. It reduces the loss of heat energy from the body to the surroundings.

How the marks are earned

One mark for saying the fat insulates. One mark for saying it traps heat or reduces heat loss. 'Keeps the body warm' is not accepted.
