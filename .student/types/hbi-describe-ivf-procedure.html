Knowledge card

Describe the steps of IVF treatment

Written
  • IVF means in-vitro fertilisation: the egg is fertilised outside the body, in a glass or plastic dish.
  • The woman is first given the hormones FSH and LH. FSH makes several follicles grow so many eggs mature at once; LH brings them to ripeness.
  • The eggs are collected from the ovaries, and sperm are collected from the man.
  • Eggs and sperm are mixed in a dish in the laboratory, so sperm fertilise the eggs and zygotes form.
  • The zygotes divide by mitosis to form small embryos, which are checked.
  • One or two embryos are placed into the woman's uterus, where they may implant.
  • Fertility hormones can cause several eggs to be released; if each is fertilised, twins or triplets (a multiple birth) can result.
  • IVF improves the chance of pregnancy because many eggs are collected and fertilised, healthy embryos can be chosen, and more than one embryo can be placed in the uterus.

Before you begin

  • The roles of FSH and LH in the menstrual cycle.
  • Fertilisation forms a zygote that divides by mitosis into an embryo.
  • Implantation in the uterus lining.
1

The method

  1. Start with the hormones if the question covers the whole process: FSH and LH given to the woman.

    Why: Hormone treatment is the first credited step; leave it out only if the question says 'after hormone treatment'.

  2. Write each step in order: collect eggs, collect sperm, fertilise in a dish, embryos form, embryo placed in uterus.

    Why: Each stage in the sequence is one mark, and order shows understanding.

  3. Say where fertilisation happens: in the laboratory or a dish.

    Why: This is what makes it 'in vitro' and is part of the fertilisation mark.

  4. For multiple births, link FSH to more follicles, more eggs released and each egg fertilised.

    Why: Each link in this chain is one mark.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Saying the eggs are fertilised inside the woman's body.
  • ✗
    Naming oestrogen and progesterone as the first hormones given.
  • ✗
    Missing the step where the embryo is put back into the uterus.
  • ✗
    Describing hormone steps when the question asks only for steps after hormone treatment.
  • ✗
    Saying FSH makes one egg divide into twins.

Common mistakes: Inferred, not measured. Inferred from the official schemes' credit and rejection patterns; not measured student-error statistics.

Worked explanation

  1. Four marks and 'describe the process', so I list at least four steps in order.
  2. Step 1: the woman gets FSH and LH so several eggs mature.
  3. Step 2: the eggs are taken from her ovaries and sperm is taken from the man.
  4. Step 3: they are mixed in a dish in a laboratory so the eggs are fertilised.
  5. Step 4: the fertilised eggs grow into embryos.
  6. Step 5: one or two embryos are put into her uterus to implant.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

The woman is given the hormones FSH and LH so that several eggs mature in her ovaries. The eggs are removed from the ovaries, and sperm is collected from the man. The eggs and sperm are mixed in a dish in a laboratory, where sperm fertilise the eggs. The fertilised eggs divide and develop into embryos. One or two embryos are then placed inside the woman's uterus, where they can implant.

How the marks are earned

Any four of: FSH and LH given; eggs and sperm collected; fertilisation in a laboratory dish; embryos develop; embryo placed in the uterus.
