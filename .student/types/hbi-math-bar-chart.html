Draw a bar chart and justify its use

Data for separate categories or grouped classes are shown as bars; may include adding an axis scale or saying why bars suit the data.

The method

  1. Axes

    Categories on x, values on y.

  2. Labels

    Y-axis quantity and unit; name each bar.

  3. Scale

    Equal steps, more than half the grid.

  4. Draw

    Ruler heights, equal widths, gaps.

    Keep every step the same size.

Worked explanation

  1. The age groups (15-19 up to 40-44 years) are categories, so they go on the x-axis. Probability goes on the y-axis.
  2. The grid is 60 small squares wide and 40 tall. A medium line comes every 5 small squares, so there are 12 medium squares across and 8 up (the heavy lines every 10 small squares are not the ones to count here).
  3. The largest value is 0.0140. If one medium square (5 small squares) is 0.002, then 0.0140 needs 7 medium squares, which uses most of the 8. Each small square is then 0.002 / 5 = 0.0004.
  4. Bar heights in small squares: 0.0010 is 2.5, 0.0015 is 3.75, 0.0045 is 11.25 and 0.0140 is 35 (7 medium squares).
  5. Make six bars, each one medium square wide, with a gap of one medium square between them.
  6. Label the y-axis 'Probability of chromosome aberration' and the x-axis 'Age of mother in years', and write the age group under each bar.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

x-axis: Age of mother in years, with six equal bars labelled 15-19, 20-24, 25-29, 30-34, 35-39, 40-44. y-axis: Probability of chromosome aberration, from 0 to 0.016 with 0.002 for every 5 small squares. Bar heights: 0.0010, 0.0010, 0.0010, 0.0015, 0.0045 and 0.0140. All bars are the same width.

How the marks are earned

One mark for axes labelled (age in years and probability). One mark for a linear scale that uses the grid well. Two marks for accurate bars (six correct heights; one mark is lost for errors). One mark for bars of equal width.

Calculation check

  • 0.0140 ÷ 0.002 = 7.0
  • 0.002 ÷ 5 = 0.0004
  • 0.0010 ÷ 0.0004 = 2.5
  • 0.0015 ÷ 0.0004 = 3.75
  • 0.0045 ÷ 0.0004 = 11.25
  • 0.0140 ÷ 0.0004 = 35.0
