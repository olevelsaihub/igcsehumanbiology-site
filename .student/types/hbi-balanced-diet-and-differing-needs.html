Meaning of a balanced diet and its components, and differences in energy or nutrients with sex, age, pregnancy, menstruation or infancy.

Knowledge card

Define a balanced diet and explain why needs differ between people

Written

A balanced diet has all the food components in the right proportions. Needs differ with sex, pregnancy, age and activity, and each needs a biological reason.

The method

  1. Define in two halves

    All nutrients, correct proportions.

  2. List five groups

    Carbohydrate, protein, lipid, vitamins, minerals...

  3. State who needs more

    Quote the table with units if asked.

  4. Give the reason

    Muscle, activity, haemoglobin, bones.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Equal amounts

    It means correct proportions.

  • ✗
    "Eat more"

    Give a biological reason.

  • ✗
    Iron carries oxygen

    Haemoglobin does.

  • ✗
    Two nutrients only

    Name at least five.

Worked explanation

  1. Two marks, so two ideas.
  2. Idea 1: what is in it? All the nutrients: carbohydrate, protein, lipid, vitamins, minerals, fibre and water.
  3. Idea 2: how much? Each in the correct proportion for the person's needs.
  4. Write it as one clear sentence.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

A balanced diet contains all the nutrients the body needs (carbohydrate, protein, fat, vitamins, minerals, fibre and water) in the correct proportions, so the person stays healthy.

How the marks are earned

One mark for including every nutrient or food group, and one mark for having them in the right quantities or proportions. 'Eating a variety of food' alone would not score.
