Locate DNA and chromosomes in a human cell

Short items asking where DNA or chromosomes are found, or which molecule or structure carries genetic material.

Type 1 Cells and tissues HBI-LOCATE-GENETIC-MATERIAL

★★★☆☆ · 5 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Easy.

Direct recall, no chain of thought. Needs the idea that DNA is packed into chromosomes inside the nucleus. No calculation. Always asked in the same way. Single idea.

Locate DNA and chromosomes in a human cell

Written

DNA is coiled into chromosomes, and the chromosomes are kept in the nucleus. Answer at the level the question asks: place, structure or molecule.

4

The method

  1. Pick the level

    Is it a place, a structure or a molecule?

  2. Cross out

    Remove RNA, sugars and other organelles.

  3. One word

    Write nucleus, chromosome or DNA, not two answers.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    mRNA is genetic

    It is not part of a chromosome.

  • ✗
    Ribose

    DNA has deoxyribose.

  • ✗
    Chromosome for place

    If asked for a part of the cell, write nucleus.

(a) (i) Which structure in a cell contains DNA? A chromosome B endoplasmic reticulum C cell membrane D ribosome

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. The question asks which structure contains DNA.
  2. Endoplasmic reticulum transports proteins. It holds no DNA, so B is out.
  3. The cell membrane controls entry and exit. It holds no DNA, so C is out.
  4. Ribosomes make proteins from mRNA instructions. They hold no DNA, so D is out.
  5. Chromosomes are made of coiled DNA. The answer is A.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

A (chromosome)

How the marks are earned

One mark for A. The other three options are structures with different jobs that do not carry DNA, so they score nothing.
