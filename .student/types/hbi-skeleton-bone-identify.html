Identify bones and bone tissues on a diagram

Written

Use position to name bones: the humerus is the one upper-arm bone, and the radius is on the thumb side. A long bone has compact bone, spongy bone and cartilage.

Name bones 1 to 4 on a diagram of the arm: upper arm, thumb-side forearm, other forearm bone and fingers. (4 marks)

4

The method

  1. Landmark

    Find the elbow, knee, wrist or thumb.

  2. Count the bones

    One in the upper arm or thigh, two below.

  3. Thumb rule

    The radius is on the thumb side.

  4. Section

    Compact is dense, spongy has spaces, cartilage caps.

  5. Spell

    Write each name carefully.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Radius and ulna

    Use the thumb to tell them apart.

  • ✗
    Humerus and femur

    Humerus is the arm; femur is the leg.

  • ✗
    Made-up names

    "Tibula" mixes tibia and fibula.

Worked explanation

  1. The model shows one long bone at the top, then two bones side by side, then the wrist, palm and fingers. Finger A is the thumb, on the left.
  2. Bone 1 is the single upper arm bone: the humerus.
  3. Bones 2 and 3 are the forearm bones. Bone 2 is on the same side as the thumb, so it is the radius. Bone 3 is the ulna.
  4. Label 4 points to the finger bones: the phalanges.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

1 humerus; 2 radius; 3 ulna; 4 phalanges (finger bones)

How the marks are earned

One mark per bone: humerus, radius, ulna and phalanges (fingers is also accepted). The radius must be the bone on the thumb side.
