4

The method

  1. Find the stimulus

    Light, temperature, pressure, sound or chemical.

  2. Name the receptor

    One or two words.

  3. Watch "other"

    Do not repeat the sense already given.

  4. Why several types

    Together they cover a wider range.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Whole organ

    Write the receptor, not skin or eye.

  • ✗
    Receptor or neurone

    The neurone carries; the receptor detects.

  • ✗
    Three cones "more"

    Link to wavelengths and colours.

Worked explanation

  1. The question describes hands in hot and cold water and asks for the structure in the skin that detects temperature. It is worth 1 mark.
  2. The stimulus is temperature. The structure that detects a stimulus is a receptor.
  3. So I name a temperature receptor. I do not write 'skin' or 'nerve'.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Temperature receptors (thermoreceptors) in the skin.

How the marks are earned

One mark for naming a receptor. The word 'temperature' is helpful but the key word is 'receptor'. 'Skin' alone does not score.
