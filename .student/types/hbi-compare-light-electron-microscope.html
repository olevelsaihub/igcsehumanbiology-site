The method

  1. Spot the focus

    Power, structures seen, or a part's job?

  2. Two ideas

    Magnification and resolution.

  3. Name small organelles

    Ribosomes, ER, mitochondria.

  4. Part plus verb

    Magnifies, holds, focuses.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Bigger or powerful

    Say magnification and resolution.

  • ✗
    Magnification gives detail

    Detail comes from resolution.

  • ✗
    Nucleus needs EM

    A light microscope shows it.

  • ✗
    Coarse or fine

    Fine is the small move that sharpens.

Worked explanation

  1. The question asks why an electron microscope is more useful for viewing cell structure. It is worth 2 marks, so I need two ideas.
  2. Idea 1: it magnifies much more, so small structures look bigger.
  3. Idea 2: it has higher resolution, so details that are close together can be seen separately.
  4. I can link these to the result: more organelles, such as ribosomes, can be seen.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

An electron microscope gives a much higher magnification than a light microscope. It also has a higher resolution, so we can see the cell in much more detail and can see more small structures, such as ribosomes.

How the marks are earned

One mark for the greater magnification. One mark for the better resolution or more detail, or for seeing more structures. Any two of these three ideas give full marks.
