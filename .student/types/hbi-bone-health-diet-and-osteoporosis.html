Explain dietary needs for bone and muscle and describe osteoporosis

Calcium, vitamin D, phosphate and protein for growing and keeping bone and muscle, and the structure and symptoms of osteoporotic bone.

★★☆☆☆ · 4 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

Two-link chains (vitamin D to calcium to bone). Simple nutrient roles. None. Food labels and photos of bone are used. Links diet, digestion and the skeleton.

Calcium makes bone hard, vitamin D helps the gut absorb it and protein builds muscle. In osteoporosis bones lose density and break easily.

The method

  1. Name

    Calcium, vitamin D, protein.

  2. Job

    Give each job straight after its name.

  3. Chain

    Vitamin D, calcium absorbed, hard dense bone.

  4. Osteoporosis

    Less dense bone, then fractures, pain, height loss.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Vitamin D builds bone

    It helps absorb calcium.

  • ✗
    Name only

    Always say what the nutrient does.

  • ✗
    Forgetting protein

    Protein is for muscle and growth.

  • ✗
    "Softer" bones

    Say less dense and brittle.

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. The label shows the yoghurt contains calcium, vitamin D and protein. All three help bones.
  2. Calcium: it is laid down in bone, making it hard and dense.
  3. Vitamin D: it helps the gut absorb calcium.
  4. Protein: it is needed for growth of bone.
  5. There are 5 marks, so I name calcium and vitamin D, give both roles, add the strength effect, and add protein for a spare mark.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

Yoghurt contains calcium and also vitamin D. Vitamin D helps the small intestine absorb calcium into the blood. The calcium is then laid down in the bones, forming hard compact bone. This makes the bones stronger and denser, so they are less likely to break. Yoghurt also contains protein, which is needed for bones to grow and develop.

How the marks are earned

Marks for: yoghurt contains calcium; it contains vitamin D; vitamin D lets calcium be absorbed from the intestine; calcium is stored in bone or forms compact bone; bones become stronger or denser; protein is needed for bone growth. Any five of the six.
