Human Biology / Identify parts of the eye and state their functions

Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Human Biology · 4HB1

Identify parts of the eye and state their functions

Naming labelled eye structures, choosing structures from descriptions, matching parts to jobs, and describing roles of cornea, lens, retina, fovea, iris and the blind spot.

Type 4 Coordination HBI-IDENTIFY-EYE-PARTS-AND-ROLES

★★★★★ · 10 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Easy.

Mostly direct naming and matching. Needs the names and jobs of many eye parts. No arithmetic is needed. Eye diagrams appear in most papers. Some tables mix eye and ear parts.

The method

  1. Follow the line

    Go to the exact end of each label line.

  2. Use the light path

    Cornea, pupil, lens, retina, optic nerve.

  3. Key words

    Light-sensitive means retina; carries impulses means nerve.

  4. Action and result

    Say what the part does and why.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Pupil or iris

    The iris is the coloured ring.

  • ✗
    Iris focuses

    Cornea and lens focus; the iris controls amount.

  • ✗
    Nerve sees

    The brain interprets the impulses.

Worked explanation

  1. The question asks me to name parts A, B and C on the eye section. It is worth 3 marks, one per part.
  2. A is at the front of the eye, the clear curved layer bulging forward. That is the cornea.
  3. B is the layer lining the back of the eye. That is the retina.
  4. C leaves the back of the eye towards the brain. That is the optic nerve.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

A: cornea

B: retina

C: optic nerve

How the marks are earned

One mark for each correct name: cornea for A, retina for B, optic nerve for C.
