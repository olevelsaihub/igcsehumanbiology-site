Classify immunity as natural or artificial and active or passive

Distinguishing natural, artificial, active and passive immunity, including antibodies from mothers or injected donor plasma.

★★★☆☆ · 5 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

A two-question decision (who made it, how it happened) applied to each case. Four overlapping categories are easy to confuse. No calculation; the graphs are read for shape only. Contexts include Ebola plasma treatment and passages. Links antibodies, memory cells, blood components and transfusion.

Classify immunity as natural or artificial and active or passive

Written

Ask two questions. Who made the antibodies: you (active) or someone else (passive)? How did it happen: normal life (natural) or a medical procedure (artificial)?

4

The method

  1. Who made the antibodies

    Own lymphocytes: active. From outside: passive.

  2. How did it happen

    Normal life: natural. Medical procedure: artificial.

  3. Read a graph

    High at once and falling: passive. Slow and lasting: active.

  4. Treating the ill

    Choose passive; antibodies act immediately.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Vaccine is passive

    A vaccine gives active immunity.

  • ✗
    Breast milk artificial

    It is natural passive.

  • ✗
    Passive lasts

    It is short-lived and makes no memory cells.

  • ✗
    Umbilical cord

    Say the placenta.

(i) Identify the different forms of artificial immunity produced by these two injections. 1 ________ 2 ________

Hint for part (a)(i)

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. Both are injections, so both are artificial. The question wants the other part of the name.
  2. Injection 1: the antibody level is high almost at once and falls to zero by about 40 days. Ready-made antibodies were injected, so this is passive.
  3. Injection 2: the level rises slowly to a peak at about 30 days and stays up for a long time. The body made its own antibodies, so this is active.
  4. Write one type per line, in the same order as the injections.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

1 Injection 1: artificial passive immunity (ready-made antibodies were injected, so the level is high at once but falls quickly).

2 Injection 2: artificial active immunity (a vaccine made the body produce its own antibodies, so the level rises slowly and lasts longer).

How the marks are earned

One mark for labelling the first injection passive and one for labelling the second active. The brackets explain the choice but the marks are for the two correct labels in the right order.
