The method

  1. Find the sugar

    The shape joined to both other parts.

  2. Phosphate or base

    Outer circle: phosphate. Inward part: base.

  3. Spell exactly

    Deoxyribose and phosphate.

  4. Say how joined

    Do not only list the parts.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    Ribose in DNA

    DNA has deoxyribose.

  • ✗
    Phosphorus

    Write phosphate.

  • ✗
    Bases as backbone

    Sugar and phosphate make it.

  • ✗
    Amino acid or gene

    The unit is a nucleotide.

Worked explanation

  1. The diagram shows one nucleotide with three labelled parts, R, S and T.
  2. S is the shape in the middle that joins to both other parts, so S is the sugar. In DNA this is deoxyribose.
  3. R is the small round group on the outer side of the sugar, so R is the phosphate.
  4. T is the part on the other side of the sugar, which would pair with a base on the other strand, so T is a base.
  5. Check the spelling: phosphate, deoxyribose.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

R: phosphate

S: deoxyribose sugar

T: (nitrogenous) base, for example adenine

How the marks are earned

One mark for each part: phosphate for R, sugar or deoxyribose for S, base (or a named base) for T.
