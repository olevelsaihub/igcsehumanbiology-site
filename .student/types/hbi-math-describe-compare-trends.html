Human Biology / Describe a trend or compare data sets and conclude

Pearson Edexcel · International GCSE Human Biology · 4HB1

Describe a trend or compare data sets and conclude

Describing the pattern in one or more data series from a graph, table or chart, comparing groups or conditions, and drawing a conclusion the data support.

Type 15 Maths skills HBI-MATH-DESCRIBE-COMPARE-TRENDS

★★★★★ · 21 of 36 eligible examination papers, 2019–2025. Difficulty: Medium.

Students must spot changes in shape, compare groups and decide what the data can support. Relationships must be stated with both variables; conclusions must not go beyond the data. Reading values and sometimes subtracting to quote the size of a change. Data come as line graphs, bar charts, frequency charts and tables in many contexts. Some items link to topic knowledge for an explanation or a conclusion.

Describe a trend or compare data sets and conclude

Written

To describe a trend, say the direction linking both variables, name any turning point, and quote data with units. Conclusions use 'suggests'.

The method

  1. Read

    Check axes, key and command word.

  2. Trend

    State the direction using both variables.

  3. Shape

    Name each turning point and where.

  4. Data

    Quote values with units.

Do not lose marks here

  • ✗
    It changes

    Say up or down.

  • ✗
    Start and end only

    Mention the peak or lowest point.

  • ✗
    Listing values

    Describe the pattern instead.

  • ✗
    Proves

    Say suggests.

Written-answer check

After your attempt, see the full worked explanation

Worked explanation

  1. The graph shows time taken to digest starch against pH. The values are pH 2: 60 min, pH 4: 45 min, pH 6: 20 min, pH 8: 35 min, pH 10: 55 min.
  2. From pH 2 to pH 6 the time goes down.
  3. The shortest time is 20 minutes at pH 6. This is the turning point.
  4. From pH 6 to pH 10 the time goes up again, to 55 minutes.
  5. So I describe two parts and name pH 6.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

As the pH rises from 2 to 6, the time taken to digest starch falls, from 60 minutes to a lowest value of 20 minutes at pH 6. Above pH 6 the time taken rises again as the pH increases, reaching 55 minutes at pH 10.

How the marks are earned

One mark for saying the time falls as the pH rises up to pH 6. One mark for saying the time rises again from pH 6 to pH 10. If the turning point is not named, only one mark is given for 'decreases then increases'.
