Written

A villus is built for absorption: large surface area, wall one cell thick, capillaries that keep a steep gradient, and a lacteal for fat.

The method

  1. Pick a feature

    Villi, thin wall, capillaries, lacteal.

  2. Link to its effect

    Surface area, distance, gradient, fat.

  3. For damage

    Villi flattened, so less is absorbed.

  4. Name the loss

    The nutrient and its result.

Do not lose marks here

Worked explanation

  1. The structures are villi. Three marks: I need features linked to effects.
  2. Feature 1: there are many villi, and their cells have microvilli, so the surface area is large.
  3. Feature 2: the wall is thin, only one cell thick, so the diffusion distance is short.
  4. Feature 3: a good capillary supply carries glucose away, so the concentration gradient stays steep.
  5. Each feature has its effect next to it.
Model answer · written by olevels.com, not an official Pearson mark scheme

There are very many villi and their cells have microvilli, which gives a large surface area for diffusion. The wall of each villus is thin (one cell thick), so glucose has only a short distance to diffuse. Each villus has many capillaries; the blood flow carries glucose away, so a steep concentration gradient is kept.

How the marks are earned

The scheme lists three features each with its effect: many villi or microvilli giving more surface area, thin walls giving a short diffusion distance, and capillaries keeping the gradient. The effect must be linked to its feature. Any three of the six points score full marks.
