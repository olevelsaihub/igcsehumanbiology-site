Read the narration

A hormone is a chemical carried in the blood to a target organ. The pituitary gland in the brain makes FSH and LH. They act on the ovary. The ovary then makes oestrogen and progesterone, which act on the uterus. In males, the testes make testosterone. It makes the male sex organs and the male sex characteristics develop, like a deeper voice. Oestrogen does the same job for female characteristics.

This graph shows the menstrual cycle, about twenty-eight days. Early in the cycle, FSH rises. FSH makes a follicle mature. A follicle is a small sac in the ovary with an egg inside. FSH also makes the ovary release oestrogen. Oestrogen rises next. It repairs and thickens the lining of the uterus, after the last period. In the middle of the cycle, there is a surge of LH: it rises very fast. This triggers ovulation. Ovulation is the release of an egg from the ovary, around day fourteen. After ovulation, the empty follicle becomes the corpus luteum. It makes progesterone. Progesterone keeps the lining thick. When progesterone falls, the lining breaks down. This is menstruation, a period. Here is the method as a table. Say where each hormone is made. Then give one strong verb. FSH matures. LH triggers. Oestrogen repairs. Progesterone maintains. Testosterone develops.

Now a real exam question. Oxytocin and LH are two hormones found in females. Describe the functions of LH. Two marks. LH has two jobs. Job one: the surge of LH in the middle of the cycle triggers ovulation. The ovary releases an egg. Job two: after ovulation, LH keeps the corpus luteum working. So the corpus luteum makes progesterone. Here is the full answer. Explanation by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

A common mistake is to say FSH causes ovulation. FSH matures the follicle. LH triggers ovulation. Another mistake is to say oestrogen keeps the lining during pregnancy. That is mainly progesterone.

Your turn. A woman's pituitary gland makes too little FSH. Suggest why she may find it hard to become pregnant. Pause here and work it out.

FSH makes a follicle mature. With too little FSH, the follicles stay small, so no egg becomes mature. With no mature egg, there is no ovulation. So there is no egg for a sperm to fertilise, or join with. Also, FSH makes the ovary release oestrogen. With less oestrogen, the lining is not built up. So every time: which gland makes it and where does it act? Give one strong verb. For two marks, give a second job. And if a hormone is missing, its job does not happen.
