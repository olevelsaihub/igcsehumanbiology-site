Read the narration

An inherited condition is passed from parents to children in their alleles. An allele is one version of a gene. So these conditions cannot be caught like an infection. We will learn four: polydactyly, haemophilia, colour blindness and cystic fibrosis. Polydactyly is caused by a dominant allele, so one copy is enough. Its effect: extra fingers or extra toes. It does not mean missing fingers.

Step one: name the main effect first. Haemophilia: the blood does not clot, which means it does not form a plug at a cut. So cuts and injuries inside the body bleed for a long time. Red-green colour blindness: the person cannot tell red and green apart. Step two: say which organs are affected. In cystic fibrosis, cells make thick, sticky mucus. Mucus is the slimy liquid on the lining of some organs. It builds up in the lungs, the pancreas, the small intestine, and the sperm ducts. Step three: give the result for each organ. Lungs: coughing, breathlessness, less gas exchange and more chest infections. Less oxygen means less aerobic respiration, so the person is tired. Gut and pancreas: enzymes are blocked, so less food is digested and absorbed. Sperm ducts: blocked, so men may be infertile, unable to have children. Step four: match the command word. State or give needs one short effect. Describe needs several different effects, each linked to an organ. Never repeat the same effect in different words.

Now a real exam question. Cystic fibrosis is a disorder that affects epithelial membranes, the thin layers of cells that line organs. Part ii asks you to describe its effects on health. Four marks. Start with the root cause: thick, sticky mucus builds up. Then name where: lungs, pancreas, small intestine and sperm ducts. That can already earn two marks. Then give results from different organs. Lungs: coughing, and less oxygen absorbed. So less respiration and less energy. Gut: less digestion and absorption. Sperm ducts: male infertility. Four different ideas in all. Here is a full answer. A common mistake is to say haemophilia means no blood, or too few blood cells. The blood is there; it just does not clot. Another is to say cystic fibrosis can be caught. It is inherited. Also, it affects the lungs scores nothing: give an effect, such as coughing. In a four-mark answer, do not list only lung effects. And polydactyly means extra fingers, not missing ones.

Your turn. An electrician has red-green colour blindness. State the effect, and why it is a problem when he sorts coloured wires. A boy with haemophilia has a tooth pulled out. Explain why the dentist takes extra care. A girl with cystic fibrosis is thin and tired. Explain both effects. Pause here and work it out.

Part a: he cannot tell red from green, so he may join the wrong wires. Part b: in haemophilia the blood does not clot. After the tooth is pulled, the bleeding may go on for a long time. Part c: thin, because mucus blocks the tube from the pancreas, so its enzymes cannot reach the food in the small intestine. Less food is digested and absorbed. Tired, because mucus in the lungs means less gas exchange, so less oxygen. Less aerobic respiration releases less energy. So, every time: name the main effect, say which organ, and give its result. For cystic fibrosis, cover the lungs, energy, digestion and fertility.
