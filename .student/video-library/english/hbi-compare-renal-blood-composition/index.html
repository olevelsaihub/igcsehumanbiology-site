Compare blood entering and leaving the kidney

Idea. The kidneys clean the blood. Renal means of the kidney. Blood comes into the kidney in the renal artery. It leaves in the renal vein. Urine leaves through the ureter, a tube to the bladder. Two things change the blood. Urea is a waste made in the liver from extra amino acids. Urea is removed into the urine. Also, kidney cells respire, like all cells. Respiration is the reaction that releases energy from glucose. It uses oxygen and makes carbon dioxide.

Method. Step one: decide which vessel goes in and which comes out. Step two: write every difference as a comparison, with more or less. The renal vein has less urea, because urea is excreted in the urine. It has less oxygen, because kidney cells use oxygen in respiration. Step three: choose a different substance for each mark, and add the reason. The renal vein has more carbon dioxide, made in respiration. It may have slightly less glucose, used in respiration. Water and salts depend on what the body needs. Step four: check which vessels the question means. Inside the kidney, each glomerulus is a small knot of capillaries. The vessel going in carries more water, glucose, urea and amino acids than the vessel coming out, because some of each is filtered into the capsule. In urine, urea is dissolved in water: water is the solvent.

Worked example. Now a real exam question. The diagram shows part of a nephron, one of the tiny tubes in the kidney that make urine. It shows blood from the renal artery and blood to the renal vein. The question asks you to explain the differences in the blood flowing through the renal artery and the renal vein. Four marks. The renal artery brings blood in. The renal vein takes it out. Point one: the renal vein has less urea. Point two: because urea is filtered out and excreted in the urine. Point three: the renal vein has less oxygen and less glucose, because kidney cells use them in respiration. Point four: it has more carbon dioxide, because the kidney cells make it in respiration. Here is the full answer. This answer is by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

Common mistake. A common mistake is to write just urea or oxygen. That is not a comparison. Say which vessel has more or less. And the renal vein never has more urea. Another mistake is to say the renal vein has no glucose. Almost all glucose is reabsorbed, which means taken back into the blood. So there is only slightly less, used in respiration. Last, do not use the same idea twice, such as less urea and less waste. And do not forget the kidney cells respire, so oxygen and carbon dioxide change.

Your turn. Ali drinks two litres of water. Compare the blood in his renal artery and renal vein. Say what happens to the water, and give two other differences, with reasons. Pause here and work it out.

Answer. Ali's blood has too much water. So the kidneys remove a lot of water into the urine. His renal vein blood has less water than his renal artery blood. Then two others. The renal vein has less urea, because urea is excreted in the urine. It has more carbon dioxide, because the kidney cells make it in respiration. So, every time: decide which vessel goes in and which comes out. Compare with more or less. Use a different substance for each mark. Then give the reason: excreted in urine, or used or made in respiration.
