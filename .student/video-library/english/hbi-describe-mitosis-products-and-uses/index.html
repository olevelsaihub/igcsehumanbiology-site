Read the narration

Mitosis is a type of cell division. One parent cell divides into two daughter cells. A chromosome is a long thread of DNA in the nucleus. Here we draw four chromosomes, to keep it simple. The two daughter cells are genetically identical. They have exactly the same chromosomes as each other, and as the parent. A human body cell has forty-six chromosomes, in twenty-three pairs. This is called diploid. So each daughter cell has forty-six too.

So when asked what mitosis produces, give three facts. Two cells. Genetically identical. Diploid, with the same number of chromosomes as the parent. Mitosis is one part of the cell cycle. First the cell grows. It makes new organelles, the small parts of a cell, and proteins. Then it copies its DNA. It grows a little more. Then mitosis divides the nucleus. Last, the cytoplasm divides, to make two cells. Why does the body use mitosis? For growth: more cells make a bigger body. For repair: new cells replace damaged ones, like in a broken bone. Some living things also use it for asexual reproduction. Asexual means with only one parent. That makes clones, which are identical copies. Do not mix mitosis up with meiosis. Mitosis makes two identical cells, with forty-six chromosomes each. Meiosis makes four cells. Each has only twenty-three chromosomes, half the number. This is called haploid. They are all different. Meiosis makes gametes, the sex cells. Some questions show a graph of the mass of DNA in a cell. When the DNA is copied, the mass doubles. When the cell divides into two, the mass falls back to half.

Here is a real exam question. Describe what is produced when a cell undergoes mitosis. It is worth two marks. Two marks, so two features. Feature one: mitosis produces two daughter cells, genetically identical to each other and to the parent. Feature two: each daughter cell is diploid. It has forty-six chromosomes, the same as the parent cell. Here is the full answer. This answer is written by olevels.com. It is not the official mark scheme.

A common mistake is to say mitosis makes four cells, with half the chromosomes. That is meiosis. Mitosis makes two cells, with the full forty-six. Another mistake is to call the cells similar. Write genetically identical. And never give making gametes as a use of mitosis in humans.

Your turn. A nucleus has six chromosomes: a long pair, a middle pair and a short pair. First, draw the two nuclei made by mitosis. Second, a boy cuts his knee. How does mitosis help? Pause here and work it out.

Part a. Draw two nuclei. Give each one exactly the same chromosomes as the parent: a long pair, a middle pair and a short pair. Six in each. Part b. The skin cells next to the cut divide by mitosis. This makes new cells that are identical to the old ones. They fill the gap and repair the skin. So, every time: mitosis makes two cells. They are genetically identical, and diploid, with forty-six chromosomes. It is used for growth, repair and asexual reproduction.
