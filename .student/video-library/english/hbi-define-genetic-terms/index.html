Genetics has its own words, and each mark needs the exact one. Start with a pair of chromosomes. A gene is a short section of DNA on a chromosome that controls one characteristic. A gene can come in different versions. Each version is called an allele. Here, capital A and small a are two alleles of the same gene. You get one from each parent.

Take cystic fibrosis, a disease caused by a recessive allele, small f. The pair of alleles a person has is the genotype. Two identical alleles, capital F capital F, or small f small f, is homozygous. Two different alleles, capital F small f, is heterozygous. The phenotype is the feature you can see or measure. A dominant allele shows its effect with just one copy, so capital F capital F, and capital F small f, do not have the disease. A recessive allele only shows when two copies are present, so only small f small f has cystic fibrosis. The heterozygous person is a carrier. Their dominant allele masks the faulty one, so they have no symptoms. But they can pass the faulty allele to their children. If both parents are carriers, each can pass on small f. A child who gets small f from each parent has cystic fibrosis. Some alleles are co-dominant. In blood groups, IA and IB are co-dominant. A person with IA and IB shows both effects together, and has group AB. Both alleles are expressed. The ABO blood groups have three alleles. IO is recessive to both of the others. So for group A, the genotype is IA IA or IA IO. For group B, the genotype is IB IB or IB IO. Group AB is only IA IB. And group O is only IO IO.

Now a real exam question. It tells you that IA and IB are co-dominant. Explain what is meant by the term co-dominant, for two marks. Two marks, so two ideas. Idea one is the condition: two different alleles, such as IA and IB, are both present. Idea two is the result: both alleles are expressed equally in the phenotype. Neither hides the other, so IA IB gives group AB. Explanation by olevels.com, not an official mark scheme.

A common mistake is to write that co-dominant means both alleles are dominant. That misses the key idea. You must say both alleles are expressed together. Another mistake is to give only one genotype for a blood group. For group A, the genotype can be IA IA. But it can also be IA IO, because IO is hidden.

Your turn. Albinism is caused by a recessive allele, small n. A woman has the genotype capital N, small n. Is she homozygous or heterozygous? Explain why she does not have albinism. Then give both genotypes for blood group B. Pause here and work it out.

Her alleles are capital N and small n, which are different, so she is heterozygous. The capital N allele is dominant and masks the small n. She would need two copies, small n small n, to have albinism. For group B, the genotype is IB IB or IB IO. And the woman is a carrier, so she can pass the n allele to her children. So for any genetic term: name the exact term. Give the condition, which alleles are present. Give the result, what is expressed. Then add a short genotype example.
